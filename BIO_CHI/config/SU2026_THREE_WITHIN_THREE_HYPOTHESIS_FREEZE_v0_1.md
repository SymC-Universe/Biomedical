## Bio Chi relational hypotheses

These are relations among the three-object architecture, not a fourth object and not a master scalar.

### B-A Concordance
Question: do scalar and modal changes tell the same directional story about realized source-native behavior?

Test:
compare predeclared scalar changes, full modal changes, and the source-native forward/reverse trajectory without forcing agreement.

### B-B Compensation
Question: can realized behavior be preserved or recovered while scalar and/or modal organization differ?

Key falsifiable patterns include:
- similar chi_bio with materially different modal organization;
- scalar return without modal return;
- modal reorganization with preserved recovery;
- recovery despite a locally amplifying or otherwise non-intuitive scalar.

Disagreement is evidence about architecture, not automatic failure.

### B-C Inheritance
Question: does pre-perturbation substrate/modal organization constrain later response or recovery?

For the current Su M397 generator lineage this is not automatically licensed. It requires an explicit carrier mapping to a source-defined pre-treatment substrate measurement or an independently qualified cross-cell-line baseline feature. Until that mapping is frozen, B-C is DEFERRED rather than inferred from the paper narrative.

## Falsification logic

The three-within-three hypothesis is weakened if:
- attributes collapse to redundant renamings of the same matrix invariant;
- chi_bio adds no information beyond the native pole description and no cross-level relation survives;
- modal composition/organization cannot be identified reproducibly;
- persistence cannot be defined without post-result mode matching;
- concordance is assumed rather than tested;
- compensation disappears under native comparators;
- inheritance lacks a carrier-qualified substrate mapping.
