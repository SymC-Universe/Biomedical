## Required P0-N record

**SEED_QUESTION**  
Can biological stability be represented by a layered architecture in which a scalar coordinate is used only when the native generator and observable license it, a modal/component representation captures organization that scalar compression loses, and a system/conglomerate representation captures nonlinear, history-dependent, recovery, and inheritance behavior that local modes may not contain?

**SEED_HYPOTHESIS**  
Biological stability may not possess a universal scalar coordinate. Instead, scalar, modal, and system/conglomerate representations may have regime-dependent domains of validity. Perturbation/recovery and inheritance experiments may reveal when each layer is sufficient, when it fails, and how lower-level organization conditions realized system behavior.

**STOPPING_RATIONALE**  
Both deep searches completed with hundreds of ranked papers and stable recurring families. Later source work changed execution eligibility and method limits but did not overturn the integrated picture: biological stability is history- and regime-dependent; native generator classes differ; no literature-supported universal biological damping scalar emerged; recovery and inheritance require direct temporal/carrier evidence. Further searching is therefore targeted to residual questions rather than repeating the broad searches.

## COMPATIBILITY_MAP

Compatible combinations require the same system, operating state, measured output, carrier/population and perturbation regime. Local generator analysis can be combined with perturbation/recovery only when those identities match. Lineage/chromatin evidence can support inheritance only when the carrier is actually measured and temporal ordering is explicit.

Incompatible or conditional combinations include:
- transporting a local mode between different equilibria or input baselines without testing;
- identifying population damping with single-cell decay dynamics;
- treating response-only data as recovery;
- promoting lineage metadata to causal carrier status;
- importing a second-order scalar into switching, limit-cycle, stochastic, nondifferentiable or otherwise incompatible generators;
- joining components across materially different carrier, population, scale, model class or measurement semantics.

## INTEGRATED_PRIOR_ART_PICTURE

The literature already describes biological stability as multidimensional, state dependent, history dependent, and often substrate conditioned. Cancer drug tolerance, inflammatory and lineage memory, NF-kB refractory/memory behavior, ERK temporal encoding, and p53 single-cell variability collectively rule out treating biological stability as an unexplored phenomenon or an obviously universal one-number property.

The generator literature also establishes that different systems require different native mathematics. Some reduced NF-kB or p53 regimes can possess identifiable complex modes. Other systems are better described by nonlinear adaptation, limit-cycle phase/amplitude, switching, stochastic channels, or higher-dimensional state-space structure. The prior art therefore already supports a representation gate before scalar construction.

What the current search record does not establish is a single operational framework that jointly:
1. attempts scalar construction only under native-model licensing;
2. preserves complete modal/component structure when scalar reduction is insufficient;
3. tests the full nonlinear/history-dependent system as a distinct layer;
4. records success, partial adequacy, regime transitions and refusals in coequal Function and Limit Maps;
5. asks what the layers mean together rather than making one layer the definition of stability;
6. connects those representation limits to perturbation/recovery and, where supported, substrate/carrier inheritance.

## CONGLOMERATED_IMPLICATIONS

1. Universal scalar inadequacy is already strongly motivated by prior art and is not a new Bio Chi discovery.
2. Representation must be conditional on native generator and operating state.
3. Recovery and memory require system history, so one equilibrium scalar cannot be presumed sufficient.
4. A local scalar may still be useful in a qualified regime.
5. The useful target is a layered adequacy map, not a forced universal decomposition.
6. Failures and refusals are informative coordinates rather than reasons to invent replacement scalars.

## CONTRADICTIONS_OR_GAPS

- Biological systems span damped, sustained, excitable, adaptive, switching and memory-bearing dynamics.
- The relation between local modal structure and realized perturbation/recovery is often regime-specific.
- Model identifiability and observable identifiability can diverge.
- Public source availability and native-method specification are uneven.
- Substrate/carrier inheritance is established in specific systems, but its relation to modal-spectrum or recovery reorganization remains incompletely mapped.
- No source in the completed search record establishes the proposed joint scalar-modal-system Function/Limit architecture as a validated biological tool.

## RESIDUAL_CLAIM

Biological stability representations should be tested as a hierarchy of conditional adequacy rather than presumed to collapse to one scalar: a local scalar may be valid in some native operating regimes, the modal/component layer may retain structure lost by that scalar, and the full nonlinear/history-dependent system may be required in other regimes. A Function/Limit map can prospectively distinguish these cases and can later test whether substrate/carrier organization predicts transitions among them.

This is a candidate integration and boundary-testing claim, not a confirmed biological principle.

## WHY_NEW_EVIDENCE_IS_NEEDED

New evidence is needed to determine:
- whether scalar adequacy survives across prospectively specified operating states;
- whether complete modal representations add predictive value over scalar carriers;
- when nonlinear/history-dependent behavior requires the system/conglomerate layer;
- whether those boundaries replicate in independent biological systems;
- whether substrate/carrier state predicts later modal or recovery reorganization.

## PROPOSED_DISCRIMINATING_TEST

After separating existing exploratory/qualification evidence from new prospective work:
1. choose one same-system native model/dataset with explicit baseline state, perturbation, output and recovery window;
2. freeze scalar candidate rule, complete modal representation, full-system reference, comparator, metrics and refusal outcomes;
3. test scalar vs complete modal vs full-system adequacy under more than one prospectively specified operating state or perturbation regime;
4. require independent observability/identifiability for scalar/modal admission;
5. treat disappearance, multiplicity or nonidentifiability of the scalar carrier as a predeclared boundary outcome;
6. replicate the boundary logic in an independent system before broader biological admission.

The failed Jaruszewicz TNF-off joint gate is not this prospective test. It remains post-result qualification/Limit Map evidence because it inherited a TNF-on carrier while linearizing a TNF-off equilibrium.
