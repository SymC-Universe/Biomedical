# Legacy-claim adjudication rule — GRI/Bio Chi bridge

**Date:** 25 September 2026  
**Branch:** `gri-biochi-bridge-p0q-20260925`  
**Status:** ACTIVE PROJECT RULE

## Rule

This investigation is not tasked with proving, rescuing, or preserving historical claims.

Every historical claim, coordinate, interpretation, and result is adjudicated on present evidence. Legacy status confers neither protection nor suspicion.

When an older construct changes status, the reason must be classified explicitly:

- **FALSIFIED:** the relevant empirical prediction was directly tested and failed.
- **NON_IDENTIFIABLE:** the available observables cannot uniquely determine the claimed quantity or mechanism.
- **CONFOUNDED:** the signal cannot be separated from a material nuisance or alternative explanation.
- **REPRESENTATION_FAILURE:** the phenomenon is present, but the proposed scalar/vector/reduction cannot faithfully represent it.
- **UNSUPPORTED:** the evidentiary bridge was never established strongly enough to license the claim.
- **SCOPE-LIMITED:** valid only on a narrower carrier, regime, or model than originally claimed.
- **DESCRIPTIVE_SURVIVOR:** the empirical observation remains valid after a mechanistic interpretation is withdrawn.
- **SUPPORTED/PROMOTED:** the claim survives the current control and promotion structure.

A rejected interpretation must not automatically erase the observation that motivated it. Conversely, preservation of an observation must not be used to smuggle back a rejected mechanism.

## Current application

For historical `chi_GRI = sigma/(2 mu)`:
- the literal identifications `mu -> omega` and `sigma -> gamma` are currently **NON_IDENTIFIABLE / UNSUPPORTED** from static TCGA-like RNA alone, not universally falsified;
- the `chi=1` physical critical-damping boundary is **UNSUPPORTED** for the static RNA proxy;
- empirical CV/2 relationships are eligible to survive as **DESCRIPTIVE_SURVIVORS** if their underlying calculations remain valid;
- native damping/oscillation claims remain admissible where source-native longitudinal dynamics license them;
- any future bridge from static RNA structure to native dynamics must be tested prospectively under the ordinary GOM promotion path.

## Operational consequence

Future experiments ask what the data support now. They do not optimize toward restoration of an earlier manuscript claim. Failures and refusals are preserved with the correct causal classification, and surviving observations are retained at the strongest evidence level they have actually earned.
