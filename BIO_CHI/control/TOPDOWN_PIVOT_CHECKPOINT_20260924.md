## Pivot

The investigation no longer assumes that scalar -> modal -> conglomerate is the construction order.

The active order is:

`Bio Chi phenomenon -> required internal/modal representation -> optional scalar compression`.

A lower-level representation is promoted only if it is required to explain, distinguish, predict, or falsify a frozen Bio Chi-level phenomenon. Failure or refusal at a lower level does not automatically invalidate a higher-level biological phenomenon; conversely, a mathematically valid lower-level coordinate does not establish Bio Chi.

## Bio Chi-level target

A biological system is eligible for top-down Bio Chi work only if the source design can identify a complete stability event with:

1. a defined carrier/system and baseline/substrate state;
2. a defined perturbation/context change;
3. a time-ordered response trajectory;
4. a defined post-perturbation outcome such as recovery, persistence, reorganization, resistance, or failure;
5. an explicit return/reference target when recovery is claimed;
6. an internal-organization observable or representation that can be mapped to the same carrier/system;
7. a native/simple behavioral comparator;
8. an independent or held-out behavioral test for promotion beyond within-source description.

Substrate/inheritance claims additionally require a pre-perturbation feature measured on a carrier that can be linked to later response without post-outcome carrier selection.

## Downward derivation rule

Only after the Bio Chi event is frozen are lower representations opened.

### Modal requirement
The modal/vector representation must preserve whatever internal organization is needed to account for the Bio Chi event. Native source models are preferred. If no native dynamical model exists, any analysis-derived representation must be frozen prospectively, must retain all relevant directions/components under its rule, and must be compared with simpler descriptions.

### Scalar requirement
Scalar compression is optional. It is admitted only if:
- its carrier is well-defined and robust to uncertainty;
- it is not a post-result selected mode;
- it preserves a predeclared Bio Chi-relevant distinction;
- it adds information, compression, or predictive utility beyond simpler native descriptors;
- refusing scalarization is an allowed result.

## Evidence states

- `BIOCHI_EVENT_IDENTIFIABLE`: the whole biological event is source-identifiable.
- `BIOCHI_RELATION_TESTABLE`: internal organization can be related to the event without outcome-based carrier construction.
- `BIOCHI_HELDOUT_TESTABLE`: an untouched/independent behavioral test exists.
- `MODAL_REQUIRED` or `MODAL_NOT_REQUIRED_YET`: determined by the frozen Bio Chi question.
- `SCALAR_ADEQUATE`, `SCALAR_REDUNDANT`, `SCALAR_UNSTABLE`, or `SCALAR_NOT_REQUIRED`.

These are evidence/disposition labels, not new biological objects.

## Current program meaning

NF-kB, ERK, and Su are retained as completed representation tests. Their asymmetric results motivate this pivot but do not determine the next system's outcome.

The next system is selected by top-down Bio Chi event completeness and falsifiability, not by availability of a convenient eigenpair or expectation of SymC agreement.
