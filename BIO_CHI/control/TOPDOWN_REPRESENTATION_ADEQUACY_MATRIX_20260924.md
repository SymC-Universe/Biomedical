## Program-level result

The top-down sequence rejects the assumption that biology should be built as scalar -> modal -> conglomerate.

Across the completed lineages:

1. A whole-event relation can be identifiable when no biological chi scalar is needed.
2. Functional recovery can occur with partial or mixed internal-state return.
3. A modal representation can be informative without being a universal behavior classifier.
4. A mathematically valid scalar can be redundant, uncertainty-sensitive, or transport farther than the complete modal representation.
5. A failed whole-event gate is not repaired by adding lower-level mathematics.
6. A passing whole-event gate does not automatically require a fuller modal decomposition if a simpler source-native internal representation already identifies the relevant organization.

## Active construction order

`Bio Chi event -> minimum required internal organization -> optional scalar compression`.

The scalar is now treated as an adequacy outcome, not a mandatory ingredient.
