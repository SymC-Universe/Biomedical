## Bridge admission firewall

A direct GRI ↔ Bio Chi relationship is testable only when the two sides can be joined on a defensible carrier.

Minimum bridge requirements:
1. same biological entity, lineage, sample family, or prospectively frozen system mapping;
2. static GRI-compatible organization measured before the target outcome or otherwise independently of it;
3. genuine ordered perturbation/response information for any temporal claim;
4. native/simple comparators for the same question;
5. explicit uncertainty/refusal handling;
6. no outcome-based carrier, feature, dimension, or threshold selection;
7. held-out or independent behavior before promotion beyond within-source P0-Q.

A cohort-level resemblance between TCGA and an unrelated dynamical model is not a direct bridge.

Where `chi_bio` is independently licensed, does frozen pre-outcome GRI-compatible organization predict, stratify, condition, or explain the scalar or its refusal state beyond native/simple descriptors?

If no robust scalar exists, B1 is refused rather than repaired.

### B2. Static architecture → modal
Does frozen GRI-compatible organization predict or condition independently constructed `Chi_bio` modal organization, modal reorganization, or mode-specific response?

- static state alone;
- simple distance-to-reference;
- native generator descriptors;
- conventional molecular predictors;
- composition/context controls where available?

No arithmetic Bio Chi score is authorized.
