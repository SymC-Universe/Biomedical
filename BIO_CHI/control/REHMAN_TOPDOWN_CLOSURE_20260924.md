**Role:** top-down whole-event stress test after the program pivot

## Whole-event result

The public POP66 CPT-11 design supports an identifiable biological event: vehicle reference, on-treatment DTP state, post-cessation regrowth with reported re-sensitization, and a long-term resistant boundary.

The prospectively frozen replacement RNA analysis found partial transcriptomic return after treatment cessation:
- correlation-distance recovery index: 0.3598;
- z-Euclidean recovery index: 0.2122;
- both exact n=3 versus n=3 label tests reach only p=0.1 because the design has 20 possible label allocations.

These exact p-values are resolution diagnostics, not promotion thresholds.

## Modal result

The frozen unsupervised primary basis retained six PCs explaining 82.86% of variance.

The DTP-to-regrowth displacement was substantially aligned with the DTP-to-control direction (cosine 0.7872), but the component pattern was not a simple rewind:
- PC1 and PC4 returned strongly toward control;
- PC2, PC3, PC5, and PC6 did not return under the frozen per-PC rule.

The modal disposition is therefore **mixed return plus reorganization**, not restoration of the original internal state.

## Held-out resistant boundary

The resistant state did not give one metric-independent RNA geometry:
- z-Euclidean placed resistant farther from control than regrowth;
- correlation distance did not.

The resistant boundary is therefore not robustly classified by global RNA distance alone.

## Genetic carrier layer

A frozen direct-VAF carrier test used the public combined POP66 Pairtree inputs and all 65 non-garbage mutation rows.

The simple carrier-persistence gate failed under both baseline definitions. The regrowth sample was not closer to the frozen genetic baseline than all three resistant tumors. Resistant tumors #2 and #3 were at least as close or closer on the primary RMS VAF metric.

Therefore functional recovery cannot be explained here as simple return to a more baseline-like VAF profile.

## Downward representation disposition

- Bio Chi event: **IDENTIFIABLE, PARTIAL RECOVERY WITH REORGANIZATION**.
- Required internal/modal representation: **SUPPORTED AS MIXED RETURN/REORGANIZATION**, but not a unique behavior classifier.
- Simple genetic carrier explanation: **REFUSED by the frozen VAF gate**.
- Scalar compression: **NOT REQUIRED AND NOT LICENSED**. No source-native dynamical generator exists in this RNA state-space branch, and static PCA is insufficient.

## Meaning

This lineage supports the top-down pivot itself. Starting from the biological event exposed which lower representations were actually needed and which were not.

The recoverable phenotype did not require:
- full transcriptomic restoration,
- uniform modal return,
- a simple baseline-like genetic VAF profile,
- or a scalar coordinate.

The source instead supports a narrower architecture in which functional recovery can coexist with partial state return and internal reorganization.

No universal Bio Chi architecture follows from this within-source P0-Q result.
