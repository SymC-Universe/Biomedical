## Scientific target and claim ceiling

Under known truth, determine whether a candidate local scalar chi is eligible for later qualification after semantic, data-contract, estimator-route, practical-identifiability, and observation/sampling requirements are separately satisfied.

This is a known-truth qualification architecture only. It does not establish biological prevalence, clinical meaning, a universal neural threshold, production promotion, real-EEG local chi, or a whole-system scalar. Scalar refusal does not erase independently licensed modal/vector Chi or system/conglomerate information.

## Generator-defined family contract

For the qualification experiment, semantic C membership is a property of the generating contract, not of the fitted likelihood.

A truth is C-member only when its generator is the prospectively frozen continuous-lineage C generator or an analytically declared observation transform proven to preserve that same latent C lineage. The exact generator implementation, parameter domain, and source identity must be bound in the packet and code manifest.

A fitted C1Q/C1Q-RS model, favorable BIC, compact profile, parameter value, or numerical stability can never convert non-C truth into C truth.

For empirical recordings, generator truth is unavailable. Therefore F cannot be promoted to PASS merely from fit quality. Real-EEG local chi remains unlicensed.

## Channel states and fail-closed rule

Each applicable channel returns exactly one of PASS, REFUSE, NEED_MORE_INFO, or NOT_APPLICABLE. Channels are not votes and are not assumed independent. No score, majority rule, compensation, or rank aggregation is allowed.

If an applicable channel lacks a prospectively frozen executable rule at packet freeze, it returns NEED_MORE_INFO and the row cannot become QUALIFICATION_ELIGIBLE.

QUALIFICATION_ELIGIBLE requires PASS on every channel prospectively declared required for that row.

## Channel contracts

### P: provenance and identity

PASS requires exact match to the frozen truth ID, generator/config identity, seed identity, duration/rate contract, code/dependency manifest, and artifact lineage. Missing or mismatched required identity is DATA_CONTRACT_REFUSAL. Incomplete provenance is never repaired from outcome content.

### S: sampling validity

For oscillatory known truth, PASS requires the prospectively declared continuous lineage to be alias-safe at every reported sampling rate and the paired coarse record to be the declared same-path transform of the fine record. For the current paired-rate design, coarse data are exact factor-2 decimation of the same generated path unless a later packet prospectively changes that rule.

Known truth with an alias ambiguity relevant to the declared coordinate is REFUSE for a local continuous-lineage scalar claim. Missing sampling identity is NEED_MORE_INFO or DATA_CONTRACT_REFUSAL according to provenance.

### T: stationarity or declared time variation

PASS for a stationary local-scalar claim requires a generator with constant frozen dynamical parameters over the declared record. A piecewise/time-reorganized generator is REFUSE for a stationary scalar interpretation, even if a stationary surrogate fits well.

An explicitly time-varying object may be investigated separately, but that does not convert it into a stationary local chi.

### F: semantic family

PASS is generator-defined C membership under the frozen family contract. Known-truth D-not-C, S-not-D, colored-memory, genuine multimode, nonoscillatory, and non-preserving observation/reference truths are REFUSE for semantic C membership. Fit behavior is not consulted.

### O: order and multiplicity

PASS requires the frozen truth object relevant to the scalar claim to contain one eligible oscillatory mode under the declared observation contract. Genuine multimode truth or an observation mapping that irreducibly mixes distinct eligible modes is REFUSE for a one-scalar claim. When order cannot be resolved under the declared information contract, the state is NEED_MORE_INFO rather than forced PASS.

### M: memory and closure

PASS requires the declared local representation to satisfy the frozen memoryless closure contract at the target level. Explicit colored-memory/extra-state truth is REFUSE for the memoryless scalar interpretation. Unresolved projection-induced memory is NEED_MORE_INFO.

### R: observation/reference mapping

R is required only where the declared observation/reference mapping can change the object or when latent response is inferred from a projection.

PASS requires a prospectively frozen transform proven to preserve the declared local coordinate or target. A nonzero scalar observation gain may be used as a preservation control when the frozen generator proof establishes invariance. Mixtures that alter modal identity or prevent unique local-coordinate attribution are REFUSE. Unresolved mapping is NEED_MORE_INFO.

### N: numerical and search integrity

PASS requires all frozen optimizer/search starts to complete, finite objective evaluation, deterministic reproduction of the selected optimum under the frozen rerun/order rule, and agreement within numerical-only tolerances prospectively bound to the packet.

Unreproduced lower-objective basins, optimizer failure, or numerical-contract violation are ESTIMATOR_ROUTE_REFUSAL. They are not semantic evidence against C.

### I: structural profile identifiability

The profile procedure inherits the qualified physical-coordinate profile construction from the Profile Function/Limit program for the same role, including its frozen grid/source identity. No delta-NLL width or admission cutoff is imported.

For this architecture, I is a conservative regular-interior eligibility screen:

PASS requires a mechanically valid profile whose global minimum reproduces the fitted optimum within the frozen numerical tolerance, whose minimum is interior to the frozen profile domain, and which does not contain a numerically tied competing global minimum under the frozen numerical equality rule.

A profile-edge minimum, unresolved multiple global minima, failed profile computation, or boundary/nonregular fit returns NEED_MORE_INFO unless the problem is a pure numerical/search failure already labeled by N. Hessian curvature remains secondary evidence and is not required for I=PASS.

This channel measures local determination inside the imposed family. It never establishes semantic C membership.

### U: uncertainty and rate sensitivity

U is required only when a row-level uncertainty or rate-stability conclusion is itself used to license the scalar. Paired-rate quantities may be recorded as descriptive sensitivity evidence without making U a licensing gate.

The current fresh v0.2 base architecture does not invent a new drift or confidence threshold. If packet-level APQ introduces a quantitative U rule, it must be prospectively justified and frozen before execution, or else U returns NEED_MORE_INFO wherever it is required.

## Applicability

P and N are always required. S is required for sampled dynamic interpretation. T, F, O, and M are required for a stationary one-mode memoryless local chi claim. R is required only for the observation/reference cases described above. I is required whenever a scalar estimate is reported. U is required only if uncertainty/rate stability is used as an admission claim.

Applicability is declared by truth class before generation and cannot be changed after outcomes.

## Refusal grammar

Rows preserve all applicable refusal provenance and may carry several labels:

- SEMANTIC_REFUSAL
- ESTIMATOR_ROUTE_REFUSAL
- DATA_CONTRACT_REFUSAL
- NEED_MORE_INFO
- QUALIFICATION_ELIGIBLE
- FRAMEWORK_NOT_OPERATIONAL

A semantically valid C truth can have estimator-route refusal or NEED_MORE_INFO. A non-C truth remains semantic refusal even when the imposed C likelihood is compact.

## Operating characteristics and Monte Carlo uncertainty

The untouched packet reports by truth class and channel:

- number of eligible rows over declared replicate count;
- false semantic refusal on valid C Function truth;
- estimator-route refusal frequency on valid C truth;
- NEED_MORE_INFO frequency on valid C truth;
- missed semantic refusal on designed non-C Limit truth;
- NOT_APPLICABLE frequency;
- multi-label refusal combinations;
- profile morphology;
- paired-rate sensitivity as descriptive evidence unless U is explicitly licensed.

For every binomial frequency, report raw numerator/denominator and a prospectively fixed two-sided 95% Wilson interval. This interval is reporting uncertainty only and is not a scientific PASS threshold.

Every optimizer failure, non-convergence, invalid result, or missing case remains in the truth-class accounting with its own state. No failure may be silently dropped from a denominator.

## Function and Limit balance

Function cases must include ordinary interior C truth, nonzero-g C truth, weaker-observation but semantically valid C truth, near-boundary valid C truth, and valid sampling/rate stress.

Limit cases must include D-not-C, S-not-D, genuine multimode, colored-memory/closure failure, explicit time reorganization, nonoscillatory truth, and observation/reference failure.

The exact packet must designate at least two ordinary interior Function classes as framework-retention controls before generation.

## FRAMEWORK_NOT_OPERATIONAL

The framework returns FRAMEWORK_NOT_OPERATIONAL if any of the following occurs:

1. an applicable required channel cannot be assigned by its prospectively frozen rule without inspecting outcomes to invent or retune a rule;
2. the semantic-family and estimator-route namespaces cannot be kept mechanically separate;
3. none of the prospectively designated ordinary retention-control Function rows can become QUALIFICATION_ELIGIBLE under the frozen rules;
4. the architecture requires a post-result scientific threshold to produce any eligible ordinary case.

This status is evidence about the architecture, not a license to relax it after outcomes.

## Cross-lane and exposure firewalls

N-B1 confirmatory cases are disjoint from N-B2/N-B3 confirmatory cases. No confirmatory result from one lane may tune the other before both freezes close.

v0.1 long-run outputs remain P0-D development-exposed. Their scientific values are not inputs to v0.5 or the future v0.2 packet. Fresh case identities are generated from prospectively frozen rules, with design lineage and access history preserved.

## Preservation after scalar refusal

Scalar refusal preserves only information licensed independently of the failed scalar channel. Possible surviving objects include modal frequencies, set-valued modal/vector descriptions, eigenspectrum, system/operator descriptors, transient response, recovery curves, or explicit uncertainty sets.

No richer representation is presumed valid because the scalar was refused.
