# NSD Fresh v0.2 Packet-Level APQ Checklist v0.2

**Status:** PREBUILT / AWAITING v0.5 ARCHITECTURE RE-REVIEW CLOSURE  
**Governance:** SymC GOM v1.0 + Continuity Hardening Addendum

Packet-level APQ cannot begin until N-B1 v0.5 and N-B2/N-B3 v0.5 clear independent architecture re-review.

## Identity and exposure

The final packet must bind exact architecture blobs, packet/config blobs, final code/dependency/environment manifest, machine evidence schemas, lane checkpoints, generator/provenance validator, exposure ledger, result-landing audit, final case IDs, and final RNG/seed rules.

No v0.1 scientific value may select v0.2 cases or rules. N-B1 and N-B2/N-B3 confirmatory IDs must be disjoint. Candidate Draft-A/B identities are not automatically final.

## N-B1

Confirm prospectively:

- C membership is generator-defined and independent of fit;
- P/S/T/F/O/M/R/N/I applicability is fixed by truth class;
- U is a licensing gate only where an APQ-qualified quantitative rule exists;
- sampling-lineage and alias-safety rules are executable;
- observation/reference preserving and refusing transforms are explicit;
- N search-reproduction and numerical-only tolerances are frozen;
- I uses the frozen cutoff-free regular-interior profile rule;
- Function retention controls and Limit classes are named before generation;
- Monte Carlo counts retain all failures and report raw k/n plus the predeclared 95% Wilson interval without using it as a PASS threshold;
- FRAMEWORK_NOT_OPERATIONAL is mechanically reachable without outcome-driven retuning;
- real-EEG local chi remains unlicensed.

## N-B2/N-B3

Confirm prospectively:

- the model suite is exhaustively enumerated;
- every representation-equivalence class is named before target computation;
- numerical tolerances verify rather than define scientific equivalence;
- exact sufficiency and observation-conditioned recoverability are separate;
- the deterministic/noiseless primary observation contract defines its compatible-model-set rule;
- any stochastic extension has a separately qualified noise/RNG contract;
- formulas and domains are frozen for spectral abscissa, physical-metric gain, peak-time/direction sets, response curves, norm-integrated burden, B-driven impulse response, C-observed recovery, and time-varying ordered propagators;
- similarity transforms bind A, B, C, G, and x0;
- matched-pair recipes identify held-fixed representation and queried target;
- switching order, phase, continuity, and surrogate construction are exact;
- global peak search plus independent reference check is frozen;
- an explicit continuous-time aliasing recoverability control is present;
- learned DMD/Koopman is excluded from the v0.2 claim scope unless separately qualified;
- conclusions remain suite/target/observation-contract specific.

## Generator/provenance validation

Before evaluation, an outcome-blind validator must verify truth labels, held-fixed identities, same-spectrum relationships, A/B/C relationships, similarity transforms, switching sequence/phase, sampling projections, lane-disjoint case IDs, and packet/code/environment hashes.

## Authority boundary

GitHub may validate identities, execute frozen cases, checkpoint/resume, hash/merge, and package evidence. It cannot determine scientific sufficiency, combine lane decisions, rank representations, choose thresholds, or promote claims. Terminal automation state is SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW_READY.

## Closure

Every BLOCKER/MATERIAL objection is adjudicated individually. Any material change after packet review reopens the affected APQ scope. execution_authorized may become true only after exact packet APQ, final implementation tests, and final identity freeze.
