## GitHub authority

The executable must be incapable of producing a scientific PASS, representation winner, threshold choice, promotion, or real-EEG admission. Its terminal state is SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW_READY.

## Checkpoint/restart

Each lane checkpoint must bind plan/packet/config/code/dependency identity, active run identity, RNG/seed state where relevant, completed case IDs, artifact hashes, incomplete case IDs, next exact mechanical action, and stop reason. Mismatch must stop rather than silently recompute or merge.

## APQ closure

Every BLOCKER/MATERIAL objection is adjudicated individually. No voting. Only after all affected authorities are prospectively corrected and re-reviewed may the packet be frozen and execution_authorized become true.
