## What happened

The target eyes-open session2-versus-session3 pair contained 59 exact matched channels. Twenty-one channels had at least one frozen descriptive peak in both sessions and were therefore eligible for the frozen first-listed-peak displacement metric.

Of those 21 eligible channels:

- 11/21 (52.38%) exceeded their own channel-specific ds003775 q95 displacement context;
- 6/6 eligible central channels were high;
- 4/13 eligible posterior channels were high;
- 1/1 eligible temporal channel was high;
- 0/1 eligible frontal/anterior channel was high.

High-shift channels spanned left, right and midline labels. The excursion is therefore not confined to one hemisphere or one electrode.

The regional counts must be read with the eligibility counts. In particular, only one frontal/anterior channel retained a descriptive peak in both target sessions, so a zero high-shift count there is not evidence of frontal stability.

## Periodic versus aperiodic/model-family structure

Among the 11 high-shift channels:

- 8 had fixed-versus-knee model-family disagreement in at least one recording;
- 4 changed frozen fixed peak count;
- 2 also had log-PSD correlation below their channel-specific ds003775 q05;
- 1 also had aperiodic-exponent difference above its channel-specific ds003775 q95;
- 0 changed zero-peak state.

This does not look like a simple whole-spectrum or aperiodic shift. Most high-shift channels occur in a region of periodic-model-family instability, but that is not the whole explanation because three high-shift channels did not carry the model-family instability flag.

The largest raw excursions require special caution:
- CP4: 23.0093 Hz, 6.89 x its parent q95, with model-family instability, peak-count change and low PSD correlation;
- C5: 14.1817 Hz, 3.71 x q95, with model-family instability and peak-count change;
- P8: 7.0802 Hz, 3.56 x q95, with model-family instability;
- CP6: 8.5093 Hz, 2.42 x q95 without model-family instability;
- TP8: 6.6616 Hz, 2.22 x q95 with an aperiodic excursion and low PSD correlation but no model-family instability.

Because the metric starts from the first listed descriptive peak and then finds the nearest later-session peak, a peak-count change can create a large descriptive displacement without establishing motion of a physical neural mode. The extreme values therefore remain Limit Map observations, not natural-frequency shifts.

## Control-pair result and the hidden session-3 pattern

The target pair is not an isolated pair anomaly.

Peak-shift-high fractions for the five same-state controls were:

| State | Pair | Eligible channels | High channels | Fraction high |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Eyes closed | session1-session2 | 47 | 1 | 0.0213 |
| Eyes closed | session1-session3 | 37 | 5 | 0.1351 |
| Eyes closed | session2-session3 | 39 | 6 | 0.1538 |
| Eyes open | session1-session2 | 35 | 8 | 0.2286 |
| Eyes open | session1-session3 | 19 | 10 | 0.5263 |
| Eyes open target | session2-session3 | 21 | 11 | 0.5238 |

The stronger pattern is therefore a session-3 eyes-open divergence: both eyes-open comparisons involving session3 have approximately 52% of eligible channels above their channel-specific parent q95, whereas eyes-open session1-session2 is approximately 23%.

The eyes-closed session3 comparisons are much lower and localize differently. This makes a simple dataset-wide session3 artifact less sufficient as an explanation, but one subject cannot distinguish state-specific physiology from state-specific acquisition, vigilance, task compliance, preprocessing sensitivity, or session-level measurement effects.

## Why it matters

The first transfer result's 4.049 Hz pair-level median was not generated by one isolated electrode. It reflects a multi-channel pattern with a strong central contribution and additional posterior/temporal involvement.

At the same time, the localization exposes a major interpretive guardrail: much of the high-shift structure is coupled to periodic model-family disagreement and, in some channels, peak-count changes. The correct next test is therefore independent-subject replication of the session3 eyes-open pattern, not promotion to a physical modal-frequency claim.
