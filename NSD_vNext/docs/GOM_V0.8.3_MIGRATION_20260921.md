### Perturbation and recovery

Perturbation/recovery is not inferred from repeat-session similarity or static spectral organization.

A future neural perturbation/recovery lane must prospectively define the perturbation, baseline state, response variable, comparator, uncertainty, and falsifier. Where the claim concerns resilience/adaptation/return, report separately:
- resistance;
- finite-time response;
- first reclaim;
- sustained recovery;
- reorganization;
- basin robustness;
- repeated-perturbation behavior.

Stability is not defined as recovery.
