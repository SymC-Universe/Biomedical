## Scientific target

Define a prospective qualification-only architecture for candidate local biological chi that keeps practical identifiability separate from semantic model admissibility.

The architecture asks:

> Under known truth, which evidence channels license a local chi coordinate, which specifically refuse it, which remain unresolved, and what broader modal/vector/system information survives when the scalar is refused?

No real-EEG admission is authorized.

## Evidence channels

The required channels remain P provenance, S sampling, T stationarity/time variation, F model family, O order/multiplicity, M memory/closure, R observation/reference, N numerical/search integrity, I practical identifiability, and U uncertainty/rate sensitivity.

Each applicable channel must return exactly one state:

- `PASS`
- `REFUSE`
- `NEED_MORE_INFO`
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`

No weighted score, compensation, or rank ordering is permitted.

## Refusal classes

Integrated output must preserve the scientific meaning of failure.

- `SEMANTIC_REFUSAL[...]`: the scientific representation is invalid for the declared local chi interpretation.
- `ESTIMATOR_ROUTE_REFUSAL[...]`: the family may be valid but the qualified estimator/search route is unreliable for the record.
- `DATA_CONTRACT_REFUSAL[...]`: provenance, sampling identity, or required observation contract is invalid.
- `NEED_MORE_INFO[...]`: the available evidence cannot decide the required channel prospectively.
- `QUALIFICATION_ELIGIBLE`: every required channel passes for later untouched known-truth qualification only.
- `FRAMEWORK_NOT_OPERATIONAL`: the conjunction cannot retain ordinary valid cases or requires circular tuning to function.

Several refusal labels may coexist on one row.

## Prospective applicability rules

Applicability is part of the design and cannot be selected after outcomes.

- P and N are always required.
- S is required whenever a sampled record is interpreted dynamically.
- T is required for stationary claims and any case with potential temporal reorganization.
- F, O, and M are required for any one-mode memoryless local chi claim.
- R is required only when the scientific object can change under the declared observation/reference mapping or when latent recovery is inferred from measured projection.
- I is required whenever a local coordinate estimate is reported.
- U is required whenever finite-sample, paired-rate, or uncertainty claims are part of the declared packet.

The exact qualification packet must state applicability for every truth class before generation.

## Hard logical refusals

Known truth outside C cannot become C because the profile is compact. Genuine multimode truth cannot be collapsed into one scalar without a separately licensed decomposition. Hidden memory/closure failure blocks a memoryless scalar interpretation. Nonstationary truth cannot be interpreted as stationary unless the time-varying object is itself qualified. Unreproduced lower-likelihood basins block estimator admission. Missing provenance blocks scientific interpretation.

## Estimator-route versus semantic failure

A semantically valid truth with numerical basin failure, profile nonidentifiability, or observation insufficiency is not evidence against the semantic family. It receives estimator-route refusal or need-more-information as appropriate.

A semantically invalid truth remains semantic refusal even if the imposed local likelihood is compact and numerically stable.

## Preservation rule after scalar refusal

Refusing local chi does not erase other information. The row must preserve every still-licensed object, including modal frequencies, modal vectors, embedded eigenspectrum, system/operator descriptors, transient response, recovery descriptors, and refusal provenance as applicable.

## Tolerance firewall

No new numerical tolerance may be learned from the viewed 72-row profile packet.

Previously frozen tolerances may be inherited only for their exact original role with provenance. Any newly required tolerance must use a prospectively separated development population, a declared error consequence, and untouched evaluation after freeze.
