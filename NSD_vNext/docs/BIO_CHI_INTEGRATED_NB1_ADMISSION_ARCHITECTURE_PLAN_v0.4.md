# Bio Chi Integrated N-B1 Admission/Refusal Architecture Plan v0.4

**Status:** ROUND-1 EXTERNAL APQ ADJUDICATED / ROUND-2 REQUIRED / NOT FROZEN  
**Date:** 29 September 2026  
**Governance:** SymC GOM v1.0 + Continuity Hardening Addendum  
**Supersedes:** v0.3 for prospective planning

## Scientific target

Under known truth, determine whether a candidate local scalar \(\chi\) is eligible for later qualification only after independent semantic, data-contract, estimator, and identifiability channels are satisfied. Scalar refusal must not erase independently licensed modal/vector \(\Chi\) or system/conglomerate information.

This architecture is an auditable eligibility/refusal protocol, not a biological prevalence estimator and not a real-EEG admission rule.

## Estimand and semantic-family firewall

The local scalar \(\chi\) is the damping-ratio coordinate of the frozen continuous-lineage C representation. In known-truth qualification, semantic C-family membership is determined by the generating truth contract, not by the fitted C1Q/C1Q-RS likelihood, profile morphology, BIC, or parameter value.

A good fit, compact profile, or numerically stable optimum cannot convert non-C truth into C truth.

For empirical recordings, generating-family truth is unavailable. Therefore this program may at most establish a qualification procedure with known operating characteristics. It cannot infer real-EEG C membership merely because all estimators fit well. Real-EEG local \(\chi\) remains unlicensed.

## Evidence channels

Channels remain:

- P: provenance/data identity
- S: sampling validity
- T: stationarity or explicitly declared time variation
- F: semantic/model-family admissibility
- O: order/multiplicity
- M: memory/closure adequacy
- R: observation/reference sensitivity when applicable
- N: numerical/search integrity
- I: profile practical identifiability
- U: uncertainty/rate sensitivity when applicable

Each applicable channel returns exactly one of `PASS`, `REFUSE`, `NEED_MORE_INFO`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

These channels are not statistical votes and are not assumed independent. Their dependence structure must be preserved in row-level evidence. No weighted score, majority rule, compensation, or ranking is permitted.

## Applicability contract

Applicability is frozen by truth class before generation.

P and N are always required. S is required whenever sampled dynamics are interpreted. T is required for stationary claims and explicit time-reorganization challenges. F/O/M are required for a one-mode memoryless local-\(\chi\) claim. R is required when changing observation/reference mapping can change the declared object or when latent response is inferred from a projection. I is required whenever a scalar estimate is reported. U is required for finite-sample, paired-rate, or uncertainty claims.

A channel cannot be made NOT_APPLICABLE because its observed result is inconvenient.

## Refusal semantics

Machine-readable row output preserves all applicable labels:

- `SEMANTIC_REFUSAL[...]`
- `ESTIMATOR_ROUTE_REFUSAL[...]`
- `DATA_CONTRACT_REFUSAL[...]`
- `NEED_MORE_INFO[...]`
- `QUALIFICATION_ELIGIBLE`
- `FRAMEWORK_NOT_OPERATIONAL`

A semantically valid C truth may suffer estimator-route refusal without becoming evidence against C. A non-C truth remains semantic refusal even when C1Q-RS fits compactly.

## Operating-characteristic map

The untouched known-truth packet must report, separately by truth class and channel:

- false-refusal frequency on valid C Function cases;
- missed-refusal frequency on designed non-C Limit cases;
- NEED_MORE_INFO frequency;
- NOT_APPLICABLE frequency;
- multi-label refusal combinations;
- rate/sampling sensitivity;
- estimator failure/basin failure frequency.

These designed frequencies are qualification operating characteristics only and cannot be presented as biological prevalence.

No single aggregate pass rate is a primary endpoint.

## Function and Limit requirements

Function cases must include ordinary interior C truth, weaker-observation C truth, near-boundary but valid C truth, and valid cases stressing rate/uncertainty without intentionally breaking the family.

Limit cases must include discrete-but-not-C truth, scalar-positive-but-not-discrete truth, genuine multimode truth, memory/colored-process truth, nonstationary/time-reorganized truth, and observation-model/reference failure where applicable.

The packet must preserve representative Function behavior alongside failures and adversaries.

## Tolerance provenance

Every tolerance is tagged as either:

1. `INHERITED_FROZEN`: reused only for its original qualified role with source identity, or
2. `NEW_DEVELOPMENT`: estimated only from a prospectively separated development set with declared error consequence.

No new tolerance may be learned from the viewed 72-row profile calibration or exposed v0.1 long-run outcomes.

Untouched v0.2 confirmation cannot share development identities.

## Cross-lane firewall

N-B1 confirmation cases are disjoint from N-B2/N-B3 confirmation cases. N-B1 eligibility/refusal criteria cannot be tuned from N-B2/N-B3 confirmatory outcomes, and N-B2/N-B3 representation criteria cannot be tuned from N-B1 confirmatory outcomes before both freezes close.

## Preservation after scalar refusal

Scalar refusal preserves only independently licensed information. Depending on the row, this may include modal frequencies, a modal/vector representation, eigenspectrum, system/operator descriptors, transient response, recovery curves, or explicit uncertainty sets. No richer representation is presumed valid merely because the scalar is refused.

## Exposure firewall

The v0.1 long-run outcomes are P0-D development-exposed evidence. Their scientific values are not inputs to v0.4/v0.2 design. v0.2 uses fresh identities generated after APQ closure from prospectively frozen rules.

## Framework failure

The integrated architecture must return `FRAMEWORK_NOT_OPERATIONAL` if retaining ordinary valid C Function cases requires post-result tolerance tuning, if semantic and estimator failures cannot be separated reproducibly, or if the conjunction becomes vacuous.

## Claim ceiling

Even a successful untouched packet can establish only known-truth qualification operating characteristics under the frozen model classes. It does not establish biological prevalence, clinical meaning, universal neural thresholds, production promotion, real-EEG local \(\chi\), or a whole-system scalar.
