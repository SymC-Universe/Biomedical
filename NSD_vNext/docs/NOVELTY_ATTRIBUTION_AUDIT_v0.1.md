# NSD Novelty and Attribution Audit v0.1

Date: 14 September 2026
Status: ACTIVE / RESIDUAL-NOVELTY MAPPING
Purpose: separate established prior art from genuinely open NSD contributions before novelty language enters the manuscript.

## 1. Rule

Novelty is not assigned to an idea merely because it was independently reached inside SymC. The forward manuscript must distinguish:

- established field knowledge;
- independently rediscovered but non-novel structure;
- adaptation or synthesis of established methods;
- candidate residual novelty;
- confirmed novelty after a sufficiently deep literature collision.

Current residual-novelty items remain `CANDIDATE` until the literature search is sufficiently exhaustive for the claim being made.

## 2. Not novel: dimensional and transdiagnostic psychopathology

### Established prior art

Psychiatry already has major frameworks that challenge one-disorder/one-category thinking and explicitly address comorbidity and dimensional structure:

- Research Domain Criteria (RDoC) studies psychopathology through dimensions of neurobehavioral functioning rather than treating DSM categories as the only starting units.
- The Hierarchical Taxonomy of Psychopathology (HiTOP) organizes psychopathology hierarchically and dimensionally from observed symptom covariance and explicitly targets heterogeneity and comorbidity.
- The general psychopathology or `p` factor literature models broad covariance and sequential comorbidity among psychiatric disorders.

### NSD consequence

The following are **not** valid novelty claims:
- mental disorders have dimensional rather than perfectly categorical structure;
- diagnostic categories are heterogeneous;
- comorbidity can reflect shared structure;
- transdiagnostic research may reveal common processes;
- a dimensional organization may complement DSM/ICD categories.

### Residual question

NSD would have to contribute a specific, independently validated **neurophysiological stability architecture**, not merely another statement that psychopathology is transdiagnostic or dimensional.

## 3. Not novel: normative modeling / healthy-reference deviation

### Established prior art

Normative modeling is already a mature framework in computational psychiatry and clinical neuroscience. It maps individual observations relative to a healthy/reference distribution rather than relying only on case-control averages.

EEG-specific examples already exist:
- normative resting-state EEG connectivity deviations have been used to predict antidepressant response;
- high-density EEG normative models have mapped spectral-power and connectivity heterogeneity across ADHD, autism, learning disorder, anxiety, and controls;
- large recent studies explicitly argue that patient-level deviations from healthy EEG norms expose heterogeneity hidden by group averages.

### NSD consequence

The following are **not** valid novelty claims:
- build a healthy reference distribution;
- express a patient as deviation from that reference;
- use EEG normative deviation to characterize psychiatric heterogeneity;
- reuse healthy controls as a reference rather than deriving a disease-specific mean.

The independent Neurostability Atlas is therefore a **methodological requirement and integration layer**, not novelty by itself.

### Residual question

A candidate contribution would be an Atlas that preserves a multilevel stability representation generated by a separately qualified Engine, including model refusal/absence states and dynamically licensed local parameters where available. This remains candidate novelty, not established novelty.

## 4. Not novel: periodic / aperiodic EEG decomposition

Periodic and aperiodic spectral parameterization is established. Test-retest work, psychiatric applications, and transdiagnostic comparisons already exist.

NSD cannot claim novelty for:
- separating oscillatory peaks from aperiodic background;
- measuring aperiodic exponent/offset;
- retaining periodic peak frequency, power, or bandwidth;
- showing that these features vary across clinical groups.

Candidate contribution can only arise from how these features are integrated with independently qualified modal, spatial, dynamical, refusal, and system-level structure.

## 5. Not novel: transdiagnostic EEG abnormalities and heterogeneity

Existing reviews and large empirical datasets already show:
- overlap of EEG abnormalities across psychiatric diagnoses;
- substantial within-diagnosis heterogeneity;
- limited disorder specificity of many conventional band-power findings;
- individual EEG deviations that do not spatially overlap uniformly within the same diagnosis.

Therefore NSD cannot use “different diagnoses share EEG features” as its novelty claim.

## 6. Not novel: neural damped-oscillator models, poles, or Q-factor language

Prior art includes:
- stochastic damped-harmonic-oscillator models fitted to human MEG alpha;
- noise-driven damped-oscillator models of visual gamma dynamics;
- direct damped-oscillator fits to intracranial rhythmic responses;
- neural-mass transfer functions and complex-pole analysis;
- EEG Q-factor measures derived from peak frequency and bandwidth.

Therefore NSD cannot claim invention of neural damping, oscillator language, resonance sharpness, or pole-based neural dynamics.

### Residual question

The open contribution is whether a **qualification and admission framework** can determine, record by record or mode by mode, when a dynamical stability scalar is warranted and when it must be refused, then integrate those admitted quantities with non-scalar neural structure.

## 7. Not novel: generic EEG classification or prediction

EEG has already been used for:
- psychiatric case-control classification;
- transdiagnostic subtyping;
- symptom association;
- treatment-response prediction;
- normative-deviation prediction.

NSD therefore needs an incremental-value comparison rather than a novelty claim based on prediction itself.

## 8. Candidate residual novelty

The following package is the strongest current candidate residual contribution. None of these items is yet labeled definitively novel.

### RN-01 — Explicit scalar admission/refusal architecture

Candidate contribution:
A formal pipeline that distinguishes dynamically derived quantities, validated operational estimators, chi-like proxies, empirical coordinates, categorical absence, and outright refusal before clinical interpretation.

Why it may matter:
Most feature pipelines force every record into a number. NSD explicitly preserves the possibility that a requested dynamical scalar is not identifiable.

Status: `CANDIDATE_RESIDUAL_NOVELTY / PRIOR-ART SEARCH INCOMPLETE`.

### RN-02 — Integrated spectral + modal + spatial + conglomerate + open-channel representation

Candidate contribution:
A deliberately non-reductive neurophysiological architecture where scalar, vector/modal, and system organization remain coequal starting representations and unmodeled information remains visible.

Status: `CANDIDATE_RESIDUAL_NOVELTY / PRIOR-ART SEARCH INCOMPLETE`.

### RN-03 — Label-blind Engine separated from independent Atlas

Candidate contribution:
A structural Engine that must qualify on known-truth and label-blind neural data before diagnosis enters, paired with a separately versioned normative Atlas that cannot tune the Engine.

Caution:
Normative models and label-blind feature extraction are individually established. Novelty, if any, would lie in the exact governance/architecture and its empirical consequences.

Status: `CANDIDATE_RESIDUAL_NOVELTY / LIKELY SYNTHETIC RATHER THAN COMPONENT NOVELTY`.

### RN-04 — Scalar-admission status as a phenotype

Candidate contribution:
Treating the presence, absence, non-identifiability, or model disagreement of a mode as scientifically retained phenotype rather than deleting the record or imputing a scalar.

Status: `CANDIDATE_RESIDUAL_NOVELTY / TARGETED SEARCH REQUIRED`.

### RN-05 — Function Map + Limit Map for neurophysiological stability

Candidate contribution:
Reporting where the architecture works and where it refuses/fails as coequal scientific outputs, including acquisition, state, model, and population boundaries.

Caution:
Model-card, applicability-domain, system-identification, and normative-modeling literatures already contain related ideas. The novelty would have to be specific and demonstrated.

Status: `CANDIDATE_RESIDUAL_NOVELTY / LIKELY SYNTHETIC`.

### RN-06 — Known-truth qualification before clinical geometry

Candidate contribution:
Requiring synthetic/adversarial recovery of dynamical parameters and false-admission/refusal behavior before disorder labels may be used to evaluate the stability representation.

Status: `CANDIDATE_RESIDUAL_NOVELTY / METHODS PRIOR-ART SEARCH REQUIRED`.

### RN-07 — Shared-instability test that requires preserved disorder-specific organization

Candidate contribution:
A transdiagnostic test where support requires both shared structure and retained disorder-specific modal/spatial/system organization, rather than treating convergence onto one latent axis as success.

Status: `CANDIDATE_RESIDUAL_NOVELTY / MUST BE COMPARED WITH RDoC, HiTOP, p-FACTOR, NORMATIVE SUBTYPING, AND NETWORK PSYCHOPATHOLOGY`.

## 10. Manuscript language rule

Until this audit reaches closure, use:
- “we test”;
- “we develop”;
- “we integrate”;
- “candidate contribution”;
- “to our knowledge” only after a documented search adequate to the exact claim.

Avoid:
- “first”;
- “novel”;
- “unprecedented”;
- “solves comorbidity”;
- “new dimensional model of psychiatry”;
- “first neural damping framework.”

## 11. Current attribution conclusion

The 2025 NSD manuscript independently converged on several ideas that are scientifically real but already have substantial external lineages: dimensional psychopathology, transdiagnostic overlap, normative deviation, EEG heterogeneity, and oscillator-based neural dynamics.

That is useful because it removes weak novelty claims early. The remaining program is more specific:

> build and falsify a multilevel neurophysiological stability architecture that never promotes a scalar beyond its model, preserves non-identifiability as information, separates structural qualification from clinical labels, and tests whether the resulting representation adds reproducible information beyond established native and transdiagnostic approaches.

That package is a **candidate residual contribution**. It is not yet declared novel.
