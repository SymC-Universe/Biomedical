4. **Dangerous dependency:** the same correct-specification family generates and fits the data. The map measures estimator sampling behavior, not model uncertainty.
5. **Competing explanation:** apparent cell-specific dispersion may be driven by information content, observation noise fraction, or finite sample count rather than a special chi/g regime.
6. **Circularity concern:** none on outcome exposure, but the inherited parameter envelope was used during C1Q-RS development. This is within-envelope uncertainty mapping, not external generalization.
7. **Missing control:** preserve a simple information proxy per truth/rate, such as truth latent fraction and cycles observed, so dispersion can be discussed without inventing a causal parameter story.
8. **Cheaper test:** no cheaper test replaces repeated realizations for empirical sampling behavior. The 2-cell mechanical preflight is appropriate.
9. **Refusal condition:** refuse any claim of biological uncertainty, universal uncertainty calibration, or causal dependence of error on a parameter from this map.
10. **Classification:** A1 MATERIAL, regional association language must be explicitly exploratory; A2 MATERIAL, distinguish estimator sampling uncertainty from model uncertainty; A3 MINOR, keep cycles-observed/information descriptors visible.
