## Conglomerated inference

The literature supports a strong caution rather than a universal ordering:

`more representation detail != automatically more useful scientific information`.

A richer operator description may be necessary for transient/recovery questions, but it can also be unidentifiable, nonstationary, projection dependent, or unnecessary for a simpler declared outcome.

The prospective research question is therefore conditional:

> What is the least representation that remains sufficient for the declared stability question, and when must the analysis refuse that reduction?

This is compatible with the GOM Function/Limit architecture and with the standing requirement to investigate lowercase chi and capital Chi/system organization together without assuming one can replace the other.
