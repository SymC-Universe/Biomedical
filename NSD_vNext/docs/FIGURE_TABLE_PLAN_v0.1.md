## Historical figure firewall

The 2025 figures are retained as provenance of architectural development but are **not reused as empirical result figures**.

| Historical figure | Forward classification | Reuse rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prodromal Critical Cascade | illustrative hypothesis | may inspire prospective design; do not relabel as observed data |
| Neuro Stability Spectrum `N=100` | synthetic architecture display | do not describe as a participant cohort |
| “Inattentive Defense” Live Neural Ticker | provenance unresolved | do not reuse as a result without source/data/transformation recovery |
| lifespan mechanical lineage | explicit simulation | may be cited only as historical hypothesis/simulation |
| substrate-inheritance comparison | conceptual visual | cannot support mechanistic inheritance |
| supplementary scale-invariance plot | conceptual cross-timescale illustration | not empirical validation |
| supplementary signal-texture matrix | illustrative waveforms | not disorder evidence |
| supplementary regional heatmaps | illustrative topology | not patient measurements |

Any visual that enters the new Results section must be regenerated from a traceable current data/code path.

## Figure policy

Every result figure must be traceable to:
1. data manifest;
2. inclusion manifest;
3. Engine version;
4. configuration hash;
5. exact plotting script;
6. result table used by the plot.

A figure is presentation of evidence, never a substitute for the evidence chain.
