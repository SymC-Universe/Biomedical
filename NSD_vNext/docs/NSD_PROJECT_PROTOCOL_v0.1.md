# NSD Project Protocol v0.1

Status: ACTIVE WORKING PROJECT PROTOCOL
Date: 14 September 2026
Governing program standard: SymC General Operations Manual v0.8.0
Scope: Neuro Stability Disorder / neurophysiological stability architecture

## 1. Purpose

This protocol keeps NSD-specific scientific safeguards local while inheriting the full GOM v0.8.0 scientific floor. It may strengthen the GOM but may not weaken MFR-14, native-model-first reasoning, epistemic classification, promotion debt, anti-circularity, refusal, reproducibility, Function Map/Limit Map, or independence requirements.

## 2. NSD System Model

The NSD System Model begins from native neurophysiology and accepted signal/statistical structure before any SymC interpretation.

Candidate state components may include:

### Spectral state
- aperiodic offset;
- aperiodic exponent;
- periodic peaks;
- center frequency;
- bandwidth;
- power;
- quality factor;
- peak count;
- complete peak list.

### Scalar starting coordinates
- validated dynamical chi where recoverable;
- mode-specific chi;
- explicitly named spectral proxies;
- broadband proxies where retained.

### Vector/modal state
- poles;
- frequencies;
- decay rates;
- mode shapes;
- subspaces;
- participation vectors;
- output-only state-space structure.

### Conglomerate/system state
- channel participation;
- mode relationships;
- inter-regional organization;
- relational geometry;
- network organization;
- whole-system observables that survive validation.

### Open channel
- residuals;
- model error;
- goodness of fit;
- rejected modes;
- zero-peak states;
- unclassified records;
- unmatched metadata records;
- failed fits and absent-feature phenotypes.

## 3. Scalar licensing

A quantity is called chi only when the native model or a validated operational calibration licenses the corresponding damping-to-frequency or stability-boundary interpretation.

For a true second-order resonance, quality factor and mode-specific chi are algebraically linked and are not independent features. Center frequency is not automatically omega_0. Peak width is not automatically a damping rate. Gaussian-fit width, broadband width, or empirically measured bandwidth must remain what it is unless a justified conversion model is established.

A whole-brain or whole-system chi is prohibited unless separately derived and validated. The project may legitimately retain a set of local or modal coordinates plus multidimensional system organization.

## 4. Healthy baseline and Neurostability Atlas

The Neurostability Atlas is an independent reference structure. It may contain healthy state distributions, age-residualized references, dataset/site information, recording condition, topography, modal distributions, scalar distributions where licensed, absent-feature phenotypes, disease cohorts, symptom dimensions, longitudinal states, uncertainty, and provenance.

The same healthy reference must not be silently rebuilt separately for each disorder merely to improve separation. Once healthy labels, encodings, covariates, and reference statistics are audited, a baseline may be locked and reused. Any later change requires a new version and justification.

## 5. Spatial preservation

Whole-head averaging may be used as one descriptive layer but is not the default final representation when biology is spatial.

Preserve where available:
- channel-level structure;
- regional structure;
- anterior/posterior structure;
- hemispheric information;
- inter-regional coupling;
- mode participation;
- whole-system organization.

Reductions must state what information they destroy.

## 6. Independence hierarchy

Trial, session, and subject are distinct units. Repeated observations must use appropriate aggregation, hierarchical models, mixed effects, clustered uncertainty, subject-level resampling, or another justified method.

No trial count, epoch count, or session count may be represented as an independent subject count.

Metadata joins must use all relevant identifiers and be session-aware. Silent `first row wins` joins are prohibited. Unmatched records are quarantined and reported.

## 7. Categorical and absent-feature phenotypes

Absence of an expected peak, mode, admissible model, or recoverable scalar may itself be a valid phenotype. Do not invent numerical chi values merely to maintain a rectangular table or continuous outcome.

Allowed states include categorical phenotype, refused, zero-peak, no admissible mode, insufficient structure, and unclassified.

## 8. Structure qualification before clinical interpretation

Preferred qualification order:

`synthetic/known-truth structure recovery -> adversarial qualification -> frozen holdout -> label-blind neurophysiological adequacy -> label-informed clinical comparison -> external/prospective validation`

Clinical labels must not teach the Engine what basic signal structure it is supposed to recover.

## 9. Detection is not prediction

Cross-sectional disorder-versus-control separation is classification/detection, not prediction of future disease, symptom trajectory, treatment response, conversion, relapse, or recovery.

Longitudinal or prospective endpoints are required for predictive claims of future state. Diagnostic separation does not automatically establish disorder-specific mechanism.

## 10. Simple and standard baselines

Any multidimensional architecture claiming incremental value must be compared against strong simple baselines and the strongest relevant native toolkit for the frozen task.

Examples may include, depending on the task:
- age/sex/site covariates;
- band power;
- aperiodic parameters;
- peak frequency/power/bandwidth;
- established EEG biomarkers;
- conventional machine-learning baselines;
- accepted clinical predictors.

Comparator selection is frozen before decisive evaluation.

## 11. Disorder architecture

NSD may investigate whether multiple psychiatric or neurodevelopmental diagnoses represent distinct phenotypes within a shared instability class, but this remains a hypothesis unless supported by cross-disorder, domain-native evidence.

Distinct disorders are not collapsed into one diagnosis merely because they share a candidate stability coordinate. Disorder-specific phenotype, symptom dimensions, developmental course, spatial organization, and native mechanisms remain visible.

## 12. Function Map and Limit Map

The Function Map and Limit Map are coequal outputs.

Function Map targets include:
- healthy occupancy distributions;
- within-subject and between-subject variability;
- session stability;
- ordinary perturbation response;
- modal organization;
- scalar/modal/system relationships;
- age/developmental structure;
- spatial and network organization.

Limit Map targets include:
- failed scalar reductions;
- missing or unstable peaks;
- strong mode mixing;
- estimator non-equivalence;
- site/acquisition non-transfer;
- poor identifiability;
- categorical phenotypes;
- out-of-domain states;
- Engine refusal;
- failure of disorder separation or generalization.

A method failure is not automatically a biological phase transition.

## 13. Claims and clinical ceiling

The project may report supported neurophysiological structure before a clinical tool exists.

Clinical diagnosis, prognosis, treatment selection, therapeutic recommendation, or medical utility require evidence appropriate to those claims and must not be inferred from exploratory group differences alone.

## 14. Reproducibility

Report separately:
- R1: release integrity/regeneration;
- R2: recomputation from preserved inputs;
- R3: reconstruction of primary evidence and provenance.

For neurophysiology, preserve dataset identity, subject/session IDs or privacy-safe hashes, acquisition conditions, preprocessing versions, exclusion rules, feature extraction versions, aggregation level, and analysis splits.

## 15. GOM migration integrity

Historical NSD-specific safeguards that were present in earlier General Protocol versions remain local obligations unless explicitly retired with a scientific reason. Consolidation into GOM v0.8.0 does not erase project-specific rules.

The historical NSD manuscript is provenance, not an answer key. Current claims are rebuilt from the present evidence architecture.
