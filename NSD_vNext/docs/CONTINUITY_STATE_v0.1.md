# NSD Continuity State v0.1

Date: 14 September 2026
Status: ACTIVE HANDOFF / RECONCILIATION REQUIRED

This file preserves project continuity without treating historical results as automatically current.

## Historical project role

NSD is the psychiatric/neurodevelopmental stability framework within the broader SymC biomedical program. Historical work treated multiple disorders as potentially distinct phenotypes within a shared instability class and used a systems-within-systems perspective. That organizing hypothesis remains scientifically testable rather than assumed true.

## Historical empirical starting points requiring re-audit

Previous NSD/ASD work included EEG-based datasets with subject/session structure and scalar spectral/stability summaries. Historical internal notes include:

- approximately 1,437 sessions in one compiled analysis;
- ASD and healthy/control groups;
- a historical scalar construction reported near unity for both groups;
- prior healthy-reference and power-analysis work;
- BCIAUT_P300 and other neurophysiological sources used in earlier exploration.

These are continuity markers only. Before entering the new manuscript as current results, each item must be reconciled to source data, code, exact subject/session counts, inclusion rules, estimator definition, uncertainty, and current GOM classification.

## Current architecture to preserve

1. Native neurophysiology first.
2. Spectral state remains distinct from dynamical interpretation.
3. Scalar, vector/modal, and conglomerate/system views are complementary starting representations.
4. Aperiodic and periodic structure are both retained.
5. A whole-system chi is not manufactured.
6. Absent peaks or modes may remain categorical phenotypes.
7. Spatial structure is preserved where available.
8. Trial, session, and subject independence are treated separately.
9. The healthy Neurostability Atlas is independent of disorder-specific tuning.
10. Classification, prediction, mechanism, and clinical utility remain separate claims.

## Immediate continuity debt

The following must be recovered or reconstructed before historical numerical results are promoted into the forward manuscript:

- exact historical NSD manuscript text and supplementary claims;
- exact code lineage from `SymC_NSD.zip` or equivalent source package;
- exact dataset IDs, licensing/provenance, and subject/session maps;
- exact definition of every historical `chi`, proxy, or spectral feature;
- whether historical peak widths came from models that license damping interpretation;
- whether historical session counts were ever treated as independent subjects;
- whether age, sex, site, recording condition, medication, and other available covariates were handled;
- whether clinical labels entered feature construction or threshold choice;
- whether the same healthy baseline was reused consistently;
- which prior results are P0-D discovery, P0-Q qualification, or eligible for later P1 testing;
- which old conclusions should be retired rather than repaired.

## Working rule

Continuity preserves questions, evidence lineage, and lessons. It does not preserve a conclusion that no longer satisfies the current scientific standard.
