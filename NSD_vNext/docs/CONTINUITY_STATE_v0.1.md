## Critical correction from recovered 2025 manuscript

The recovered paper is best understood as an **architecture-first P0-D document**, not an empirical validation paper.

Its plots successfully showed what the proposed architecture could look like, but the available sources do not provide the data lineage, code, participant tables, preprocessing chain, or inference path required to treat the plotted disorder coordinates and thresholds as measured results.

Consequently:
- historical figures are demonstrations unless specific provenance is later recovered;
- `N=100` in the historical Neuro Stability Spectrum is not accepted as an empirical cohort count;
- the “Live Neural Ticker” cannot support a result without recoverable dataset/subject/transformation provenance;
- the lifespan lineage is explicitly a simulation;
- the quantum-to-neural figure is conceptual and cannot establish substrate inheritance;
- supplementary heatmaps and signal textures are architecture illustrations, not patient-level evidence.

This correction is now part of the permanent continuity record rather than something to be rediscovered later.

## Current architecture to preserve

1. Native neurophysiology first.
2. Spectral state remains distinct from dynamical interpretation.
3. Scalar, vector/modal, and conglomerate/system views are complementary starting representations.
4. Aperiodic and periodic structure are both retained.
5. A whole-system chi is not manufactured.
6. Absent peaks or modes may remain categorical phenotypes.
7. Spatial structure is preserved where available.
8. Trial, session, and subject independence are treated separately.
9. The healthy Neurostability Atlas is independent of disorder-specific tuning.
10. Classification, prediction, mechanism, and clinical utility remain separate claims.
11. Architecture visualization is not evidence until linked to audited data and licensed estimators.

## Literature collision update

A deeper literature pass establishes two important corrections:

1. damped-oscillator and complex-pole models of neural rhythms have genuine prior art in MEG, LFP, sEEG, and neural-mass modeling;
2. aperiodic exponent is not a universally reliable direct measure of excitation/inhibition and therefore cannot serve as a direct historical chi mapping.

This strengthens the forward architecture: neural damping remains a legitimate model-conditional target, but only where a generative model earns it.

## Working rule

Continuity preserves questions, evidence lineage, architectural insight, and lessons. It does not preserve a conclusion that no longer satisfies the current scientific standard.

## Checkpoint auto-continuation rule — 25 September 2026

A research checkpoint is a durable recovery point, not a request for new permission.

When a checkpoint closes and the next step is already scientifically justified, previously authorized, and does not require a new scientific choice, execution continues automatically. The executor must:

1. write the checkpoint and its scientific ceiling;
2. audit failures, anomalies, and outliers before promotion;
3. repair purely mechanical or infrastructure blockers without waiting for user prompting when frozen science is unchanged;
4. continue into the next already-justified experiment;
5. stop only when a genuine scientific decision, new claim boundary, new data commitment requiring approval, or other user-level choice is reached.

A failed checkpoint does not terminate the line. The failure is preserved as evidence and investigated for root cause, reproducibility, distributional/outlier status, and separation of implementation/infrastructure causes from genuine data/model behavior before the next action is chosen.

This rule applies to the current ds004148 sequence: corrected independent descriptive transfer -> spatial/state localization -> additional untouched-subject replication if the localization result remains interpretable and the next rung does not require a new scientific choice.
