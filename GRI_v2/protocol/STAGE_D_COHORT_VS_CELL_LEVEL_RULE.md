# Stage D Cohort-vs-Cell-Level Rule

Bulk/public datasets may contain cell populations or replicate cultures rather than single-cell longitudinal trajectories. Stage D must report the actual experimental unit.

A population-level recovery measurement may support population-level regulatory recovery/persistence. It cannot be silently promoted to a statement that every individual cell crossed or failed a stability boundary.

Single-cell or lineage-resolved claims require corresponding data and a separately frozen analysis.
