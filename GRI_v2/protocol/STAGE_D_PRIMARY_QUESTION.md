# Stage D Primary Question

Can a prospectively defined methylation parent architecture, measured before or at an acute perturbation state, predict independently measured later RNA/chromatin recovery or persistence after perturbation withdrawal better than generic, scrambled, and capacity-matched controls, and do any recovery-associated signatures generalize without refitting to independent experimentally ordered malignant-transformation/resistance systems?

This is the Stage-D scientific north star. Secondary analyses cannot replace it after outcomes are known.
