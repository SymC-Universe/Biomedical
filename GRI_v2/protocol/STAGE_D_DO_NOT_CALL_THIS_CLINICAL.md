# Stage D Clinical-Language Boundary

Until separately validated in an independent clinical protocol, Stage D may not be described as a clinical diagnostic, prognostic test, treatment-response predictor, screening assay, or clinical decision-support tool.

The intended developmental target is a preclinical cellular recovery/resilience-failure diagnostic. Even that label becomes empirical only if perturbation-recovery data validate recovery prediction. Before then, use `candidate recovery-assessment framework` or the exact gate-specific claim.
