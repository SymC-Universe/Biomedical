# Stage D Biological Interpretation Firewall

The following distinctions are mandatory:

- methylation predicts RNA recovery != methylation causes RNA recovery;
- methylation perturbation changes RNA != inherited causal mechanism is established;
- D1 recovery association != malignant transformation is explained;
- D3/D4 generalization != inability to recover causes cancer;
- cross-layer alignment != substrate identity;
- P0 directional prediction != causal direction;
- RPPA coupling demonstrates generic cross-assay coordination is a live alternative;
- direct intervention plus specificity is required before stronger inheritance language becomes eligible.
