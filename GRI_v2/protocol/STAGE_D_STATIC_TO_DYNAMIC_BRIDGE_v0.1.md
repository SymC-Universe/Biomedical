# Stage D static-to-dynamic bridge v0.1

Status: CONCEPTUAL BRIDGE FROZEN BEFORE STAGE-D BIOLOGICAL OUTCOME INSPECTION

## Current evidence

The completed upstream GRI program establishes static and predictive-transport facts only:
- reproducible methylation organization above a construction floor;
- same-patient methylation/RNA geometry above patient shuffling;
- patient-specific Hallmark coupling above patient shuffling;
- a small, support-sensitive same-label Hallmark advantage;
- internal held-out methylation-to-RNA predictive information beyond two prespecified composition covariates;
- a frozen global-geometry confidence coordinate that prospectively ranked FINAL_HOLDOUT prediction risk.

None of those facts establishes recovery, restoring rate, temporal memory, critical slowing, loss of resilience, or malignant-transition causality.

## Stage-D target

Stage D introduces actual perturbation and withdrawal data so that the primary biological object becomes return/persistence after perturbation removal rather than static similarity.

The target questions are:
1. Does the system return toward its matched pre-perturbation/control state?
2. How much displacement persists after perturbation removal?
3. Where multiple post-withdrawal times exist, is a recovery trajectory identifiable?
4. Which parent methylation structures predict later child RNA/chromatin persistence?
5. Do those predictors generalize to independently ordered transformation/resistance systems?

## Dynamical quantities that may become admissible later

A restoring-rate or recovery-time interpretation requires multiple time points and an identifiable dynamical model. If a local return trajectory is adequately described by

    d(t) approximately A exp(-k t) + d_infinity,

then k is an empirical restoring rate and tau = 1/k is a recovery time only for that fitted representation and condition.

These quantities are refused when:
- time support is inadequate;
- the chosen model is not identifiable;
- a persistent-offset/nonexponential model fits better under the frozen comparison;
- residual structure violates the model assumptions;
- uncertainty spans qualitatively different recovery behaviors.

## No premature mapping of static coordinates

Stage D explicitly does not assume:
- high S_spec means rigid or malignant;
- low S_spec means flexible or healthy;
- high CKA means low resilience;
- low CKA means high resilience;
- H3a variance is critical slowing;
- a fixed weighted combination of S_spec, CKA, and H3a is a stability index.

Those mappings become eligible only if perturbation-recovery outcomes establish them prospectively.

## Multi-view endpoint

The intended mature diagnostic, if evidence eventually supports it, reports:
- modal recovery state;
- scalar compressed recovery coordinate only if empirically licensed;
- conglomerated program/subsystem recovery;
- predictive risk of future failed recovery;
- confidence/refusal status.

Stage D v0.1 is the first prospective attempt to validate those relationships. It is not itself a declaration that such a diagnostic already exists.
