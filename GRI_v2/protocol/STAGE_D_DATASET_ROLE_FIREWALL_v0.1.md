# Stage D Dataset Role Firewall v0.1

The four frozen datasets have different evidentiary roles and may not be pooled as if they were interchangeable replicates.

- D1 (GSE216989/GSE216986): direct perturbation-withdrawal-recovery; primary intervention/recovery role.
- D2 (GSE20945): temporal perturbation/recovery replication; primary time-structure role.
- D3 (GSE231780/GSE231778): experimentally ordered malignant-transformation challenge; no direct recovery claim.
- D4 (GSE91071/GSE91069): transformation-resistance/contrast challenge; no direct recovery claim.

Forbidden pooling:
- D3/D4 cannot be averaged with D1/D2 to manufacture a recovery effect.
- D1/D2 cannot be used as malignant-transformation chronology.
- Dataset role cannot be changed after results because another role is more favorable.

Cross-dataset synthesis occurs only after dataset-specific results are frozen and reported.
