# Stage D Claim Ladder in Plain Language

D-P1: The perturbation actually changed the methylation parent representation.

D-P2: That changed methylation representation predicted a later RNA/chromatin state better than the frozen controls.

D-P3: The prediction was not erased by the frozen technical/context attacks.

D-P4: After the perturbation was removed, we could measure return versus persistence, and the parent representation predicted that recovery behavior.

D-P5: A recovery-associated signature frozen from D1/D2 also behaved as predicted in an independent experimentally ordered transformation/resistance experiment.

D-P6: Generic shared-state and scrambled/capacity-matched alternatives did not explain the result equally well.

Only the gates actually passed may be claimed. Even D-P6 would still be evidence in the tested models, not proof of a universal cancer law or a clinical diagnostic.
