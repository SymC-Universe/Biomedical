# Stage D No-Result-Tuning Rule

Once any Stage-D biological matrix is opened for outcome analysis:

- no new primary endpoint may be introduced to rescue an unfavorable result;
- no feature universe may be changed because a different universe performs better;
- no dataset may be dropped because its direction is unfavorable;
- no control/null may be weakened because it erases an effect;
- no nonlinear method may be selected because it produces significance;
- no transformation ordering may be altered to improve monotonicity;
- no context depth may be selected because it maximizes correspondence;
- no scalar combination may be fitted and then described as preregistered.

Permitted after-result additions are limited to:
1. mechanical repairs that leave frozen science unchanged;
2. explicitly labeled descriptive diagnostics needed to understand a failure;
3. future hypotheses that cannot upgrade the frozen Stage-D result.

All favorable and unfavorable frozen outputs must be preserved in the Stage-D ledger and manuscript integration record.
