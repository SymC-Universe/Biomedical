# Stage D Success Is Not Required

The Stage-D program is designed so a scientifically useful result can be:
- perturbation with no predictive transport;
- predictive transport with no recovery association;
- recovery association that does not generalize to transformation;
- nonlinear-only support;
- assay/context-specific support;
- influence only;
- complete refusal or null.

The manuscript extension is only promoted as far as the evidence reaches. An unfavorable Stage-D outcome does not authorize changing the frozen question, dataset role, or controls.
