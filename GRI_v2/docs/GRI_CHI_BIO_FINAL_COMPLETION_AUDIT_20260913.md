## 1. Final G1 restoration disposition

### What the literature supports

Gene-regulatory ODE/Jacobian models commonly separate regulatory production/interactions from transcript degradation. In such models the degradation term contributes the negative diagonal/restoration component, while regulatory interactions occupy the remaining Jacobian structure. Genome-wide RNA-stability methods such as BRIC-seq can estimate transcript-specific half-lives and therefore, in principle, provide the kind of independently measured decay information required to constrain a restoration operator.

Representative sources:

- Chen et al., *Gene regulatory network stabilized by pervasive weak repressions: microRNA functions revealed by the May-Wigner theory*, which explicitly treats transcript decay rates as diagonal GRN terms and separates them from off-diagonal regulation (PMCID: PMC8291590).
- Imamachi et al., *BRIC-seq: a genome-wide approach for determining RNA stability in mammalian cells* (PMID: 23872059), which describes transcriptome-wide half-life measurement by BrU pulse-chase sequencing.
- Tani et al., *Genome-wide determination of RNA stability reveals hundreds of short-lived noncoding transcripts in mammals* (PMCID: PMC3337439), which reports transcriptome-scale mammalian RNA half-lives.
- The SCC25-specific literature includes direct transcript-stability measurements, including E2F1 mRNA stability in SCC25 (PMID: 9563476), showing that transcript decay is biologically measurable in this cell line.

### What the literature does not supply

The targeted source search did not identify a genome-wide, SCC25-matched, condition-compatible transcript-decay dataset sufficient to estimate the restoration operator required by the normalized empirical G1 route used in this program.

The isolated SCC25 measurements are biologically relevant but do not identify the multi-gene restoration structure. Generic mammalian or other-cell-line half-life atlases cannot simply be transplanted as SCC25 restoration values without introducing an unvalidated assumption capable of determining the normalization itself.

Likewise, setting a common decay constant from literature would recreate the exact failure mode C3 was designed to prevent: a convenient scalar restoration term would manufacture a unity normalization rather than independently measure it.
