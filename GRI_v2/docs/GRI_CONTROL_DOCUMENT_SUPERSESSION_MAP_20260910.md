## Current consequences

### Chi convention

No biological chi is currently admitted in GRI. If a future GRI dynamical chi uses the canonical second-order construction, it must identify a licensed factor and the proper undamped natural frequency. A convenient rate ratio does not inherit the chi=1 critical boundary.

### Scalar/modal/conglomerate architecture

GRI currently has all three starting representation classes available, but the General Protocol no longer treats those as an exhaustive decomposition of chi or as mandatory for every future domain. In GRI they remain useful because the native statistical architecture actually supports them.

### Regulatory autonomy

Any historical `candidate_autonomy_score` or discussion of retaining a regulatory-autonomy scalar is development ancestry only. F4's later `AUTONOMY_REDUNDANT_NARROW` decision governs current scope. Transparent predictability and shared/private structure may remain; autonomy is not an admitted independent output.

### Publication versus tool qualification

The current static-architecture paper may close at its smaller defensible evidence ceiling after its pending sensitivity/comparator/final-audit work. It need not masquerade as a completed Predictive GRI Tool. External P1/P2 qualification is required before broader predictive/tool claims, not before a narrower supported static empirical paper can exist.

### Function/Limit balance

All future development/qualification work adds the v0.7.1A Function Map and Limit Map obligations. Historical results retain their original status and are mapped descriptively without promotion.

## Rule for future stale descriptions

When an older GRI file conflicts with the current protocol:

- do not delete or silently rewrite the historical evidence;
- identify the exact old clause;
- identify the superseding current rule;
- use the current rule prospectively;
- update only mutable current-facing descriptions or add a versioned supersession notice;
- preserve hashes/freeze identity of historically frozen files.

**Current disposition:** old control records remain auditable ancestry; current prospective authority is unambiguous.
