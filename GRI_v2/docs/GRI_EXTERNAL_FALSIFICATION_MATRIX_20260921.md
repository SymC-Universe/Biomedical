## Candidate source strengths and limits

### Prostate GSE262522 + GSE262524 + GSE237995

**Strength:** exact 121/121 methylation↔RNA title bijection across tumor/adjacent tissue and two methylation platforms.

**Best current role:** clean external static cross-omic transport and platform sensitivity.

**Limit:** not a time course; cannot establish recovery, dynamics, or a dynamical scalar.

### Breast GSE58999 + GSE57968

**Strength:** source-declared matched primary/metastasis pairs with 36 expression-paired patients inside 44 methylation pairs.

**Best current role:** paired state-change Function/Limit mapping.

**Limit:** per-sample production crosswalk still needs a final mechanical export; two states are not a continuous trajectory.

### Nomura IDH glioma 2026 dual capture

**Strength:** genuine longitudinal tumors and same-nucleus XRBS methylation + Smart-seq2 RNA, 2,117 jointly measured nuclei.

**Best current role:** strongest currently qualified representation challenge to bulk TCGA cross-layer architecture; can ask what bulk reduction loses.

**Limit:** requires a native single-nucleus adapter. It must not be forced through a TCGA-array representation.

### Melanoma MAPKi GSE65186

**Strength:** 18 patients with shared baseline plus at least one shared post-resistance methylation/transcriptome state; additional cell-model paired states.

**Best current role:** ordered resistance-state Function/Limit and transport test after manifest completion.

**Limit:** discrete biopsy states do not establish continuous dynamics.

### CARE IDH 2026

**Strength:** 35 longitudinal patients, 75 tumors; 22 exact matched longitudinal RNA+ATAC Multiome pairs; richer treatment/genetic context.

**Best current role:** independent ordered state-change and genetic/contextual architecture.

**Limit:** exact public DNA-methylation overlap remains unresolved, so it cannot yet serve as a clean methylation↔RNA replication source.

## Investigation rule from here

A material GRI claim is no longer marked "supported" solely because:

- code passes;
- arithmetic is correct;
- the null is internally well constructed;
- multiple internal analyses agree;
- the same TCGA family reproduces it.

The strongest permissible internal label is **internally supported under the tested design**.

External promotion requires a same-question, frozen, independent test. Literature determines what the test must respect; external data determine whether the GRI result survives reality.
