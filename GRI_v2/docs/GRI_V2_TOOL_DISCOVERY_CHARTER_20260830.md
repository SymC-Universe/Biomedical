# GRI v2 Biomedical Tool-Discovery Charter

**STATUS: LOCKED PROGRAM OBJECTIVE — DOES NOT MODIFY FROZEN STAGE C1 v2 SCIENCE**

Date locked: 2026-08-30

## 1. Program objective

The GRI biomedical program is no longer organized around defending the historical GRI manuscript, recovering a historical `CV/2` scalar, validating a universal cancer `chi`, or preserving any specific SymC interpretation.

The historical work is **research ancestry**. It supplies observations, candidate mathematics, falsifiable questions, failure modes, and possible tool concepts. None of its claims receives evidential priority.

The program objective is:

> Use legitimate mathematics, prospective computation, adversarial nulls, and independent data to discover and validate biomedical tools that give researchers useful information beyond appropriate existing methods.

The operating sequence is:

**observation -> legitimate mathematics -> computation -> adversarial comparison -> useful scientific tool**

not:

**historical claim -> accumulate supporting evidence**.

A useful method that contains no `chi`, no damping language, and no "stability" label is a complete success if it survives prospective validation and improves a scientifically relevant task.

## 2. Four inherited ideas worth carrying forward

The following concepts are retained as research questions, not established mechanisms.

### 2.1 State rather than mutation alone

The original GRI sought to characterize the current functional condition of a tumor rather than treat molecular driver identity as a complete description of system behavior.

Modern form:

> Can a tumor's regulatory and multi-omic architecture provide reproducible information about its current functional organization beyond conventional mutation, subtype, composition, or other standard descriptors?

No historical GRI zone, threshold, or `chi` state is presumed valid.

### 2.2 Screen one layer, independently verify another

Single-layer structure is not sufficient for a cross-layer or mechanistic claim.

Modern form:

1. establish organization within one molecular layer;
2. test an independently measured second layer;
3. attack the apparent relationship with patient-alignment, semantic-label, technical, and measured-confounder nulls;
4. treat discordance as evidence against the proposed cross-layer interpretation rather than rationalizing it away.

This becomes the backbone of the cross-omic architecture / integration-readiness tool concept.

### 2.3 Regulatory autonomy

The most valuable descendant of historical "substrate capture" is not a claim that one biological layer literally captures another. It is the measurable question:

> How much organization in a regulatory layer remains independent after conditioning on a candidate substrate/context layer and known confounders?

The program will develop explicit **regulatory-autonomy / substrate-conditioned-dependence** measurements only if they can be distinguished from ordinary correlation, generic shared latent factors, common confounding, and trivial predictability.

A legitimate autonomy statistic must preserve modal traceability and system-level interpretation. It cannot be defined by choosing the formulation that best fits TCGA after results are seen.

### 2.4 Recoverability / adaptive reserve

The most defensible descendant of historical "control bandwidth" is a future dynamic quantity:

> How much perturbation can a biological system tolerate while retaining or recovering its prior organization?

This concept is **not licensed by static PanCanAtlas data**. It requires ordered perturbation, longitudinal, or time-course measurements.

Candidate future observables include response amplitude, recovery time, return-to-baseline error, hysteresis, restoration of modal structure, and restoration/loss of regulatory autonomy.

## 3. Permanent landscape-versus-trajectory firewall

The program will keep static architecture and true dynamics separate.

### Landscape / state map

May include:

- within-layer eigenspectra and modal contributions;
- explicit scalar summaries derived from modal structure;
- pathway/module/conglomeration topology;
- cross-layer geometry;
- regulatory autonomy or conditional dependence;
- composition/context sensitivity;
- technical robustness.

### Trajectory / dynamic map

Requires ordered data and may include:

- temporal state changes;
- perturbation response;
- recovery;
- hysteresis;
- mode switching;
- loss or regain of autonomy;
- transition-risk / early-warning behavior.

Static rank, gene ordering, or cross-sectional cohort geometry may never be promoted into temporal progression by interpretation alone.

## 4. Current candidate tool

The strongest near-term candidate is a **cross-omic architecture / integration-readiness auditor**.

Researcher-facing question:

> I have matched molecular layers. Is there statistically defensible shared organization worth integrating, what carries it, how specific is it, how much autonomy remains in each layer, and does the result survive construction, confounding, technical, semantic, and patient-alignment attacks?

A minimum useful tool should report:

1. **Organization** — is each layer structured beyond construction-aware nulls?
2. **Coupling** — do the same samples share multivariate geometry across layers?
3. **Semantic specificity** — does coupling occur between biologically corresponding modules rather than arbitrary labels?
4. **Autonomy** — how much regulatory organization remains independent after conditioning on substrate/context and measured confounders?
5. **Technical robustness** — does the conclusion survive prespecified filtering/mask tracks?
6. **Traceability** — which modes, features, genes, and modules carry the result?
7. **Refusal state** — does the evidence fail to justify integration or cross-layer interpretation?

The tool is not required to fuse assays. Its first job is to determine whether fusion or shared biological interpretation is warranted.

## 5. Methodological decision states

Candidate reporting states are methodological, not disease classes:

- `NO_SHARED_STRUCTURE`
- `WITHIN_LAYER_ONLY`
- `GLOBAL_SHARED_ONLY`
- `SEMANTIC_SHARED_CONFOUNDED`
- `SEMANTIC_SHARED_ROBUST`

An autonomy dimension may later refine these states only after its estimator passes independent synthetic and baseline validation.

## 6. Role of modal + scalar + conglomeration

The three views remain complementary:

- **modal:** mode-resolved carrier of the observed structure;
- **scalar:** explicit compression of part of that carrier when compression is scientifically useful;
- **conglomeration:** module/network/system-level organization.

No scalar replaces the modal carrier. Conglomeration does not substitute for mode structure. A scalar may be discarded entirely if it adds no scientific utility.

No current static scalar is biological `chi`.

## 7. What is explicitly not inherited as an assumption

The program does not assume:

- `CV/2` is biological damping or `chi`;
- mean expression is a physical frequency/energy coordinate;
- expression SD is a dissipation rate;
- `chi=1` is a cancer optimum, healthy state, or treatment target;
- historical GRI zones are biological states;
- gene rank is time;
- chromatin accessibility or methylation is "damping";
- historical substrate capture is established;
- control bandwidth has been measured from static RNA;
- exceptional-point crossing is a cancer treatment mechanism;
- historical executor rankings identify causal therapeutic targets;
- mobilize/stabilize/protect is a validated treatment rule.

Any descendant of these ideas must be redefined using independently measurable quantities and prospective tests.

## 8. Current Stage C1 relationship

Stage C1 v2 is already frozen prospectively and remains unchanged.

Canonical preregistration:

`docs/STAGE_C1_ANALYSIS_PREREGISTRATION_20260830.md`

C1 remains valuable because it prospectively tests:

- methylation organization beyond exact probe marginals;
- global methylation/RNA sample geometry;
- Hallmark-specific cross-layer coupling;
- purity/leukocyte sensitivity;
- publication-faithful versus technical-mask robustness.

However, **full C1 biological execution is intentionally paused behind the tool-feasibility gate**. The program will first determine whether the general mathematical architecture can become a useful method and whether an explicit regulatory-autonomy statistic is feasible.

C1 is not retuned by feasibility work. If the candidate tool fails feasibility testing, C1 may still be run later to close its preregistered scientific question, but the program will not build a large tool around a failed architecture.

## 9. Required sequence from here

### Gate F0 — lock program objective

Status: **PASS with this charter**.

### Gate F1 — mathematical kernel

Implement general, assay-agnostic functions for:

- marginal-preserving within-layer organization tests;
- eigenspectrum/effective-rank/participation diagnostics;
- mode-to-feature contribution tracing;
- CKA / principal-angle cross-layer geometry;
- patient-alignment and semantic-label nulls;
- covariate projection;
- candidate autonomy/conditional-dependence estimators;
- explicit refusal outputs.

No C1 beta-value biological result may be inspected during F1.

### Gate F2 — synthetic ground-truth challenge suite

Required cases include:

1. independent layers with matched-looking marginals;
2. one genuine shared mode;
3. several shared plus layer-specific modes;
4. false sharing generated only by a common confounder;
5. shared global geometry with scrambled biological labels;
6. module-specific sharing with weak global alignment;
7. technical outliers manufacturing false concordance;
8. unequal feature counts and missingness;
9. nonlinear sharing that the initial linear method should refuse or under-detect rather than overclaim;
10. high apparent coupling with preserved regulatory autonomy;
11. high apparent coupling with low autonomy caused by a substrate-conditioned shared driver;
12. confounded apparent loss of autonomy that should disappear after correct covariate control.

### Gate F3 — baseline competition

The proposed method must be compared against the relevant capabilities of established approaches. The objective is not to beat every baseline at its own task. The candidate must demonstrate a useful decision, decomposition, calibration, refusal behavior, or interpretability advantage not already supplied adequately.

### Gate F4 — tool decision

Outcome must be one of:

- **GO** — architecture and autonomy concepts provide a defensible useful capability;
- **NARROW** — retain only the component that adds value;
- **STOP** — do not develop a standalone tool from this architecture.

This decision is made before expensive expansion around unrevealed C1 biology.

### Gate C1 — first prospective biological case study

Only after F1-F4 justify continued development do we invest in the complete frozen C1 TCGA execution path.

C1 outcomes remain interpreted under their existing preregistration and claim ceilings.

### Gate D — dynamic extension

Only after a static method proves useful do we seek genuine perturbational/time-course data to test recoverability, transition risk, and any descendant of bandwidth/inheritance concepts.

### Gate V — held-out / external validation

Any promoted tool must be frozen and tested on data not used to select its architecture.

## 10. Kill criteria

Stop or narrow the tool if:

- null refusal is poorly calibrated;
- confounding is routinely misclassified as robust sharing;
- semantic specificity cannot be distinguished from generic cross-layer geometry;
- autonomy collapses to ordinary correlation or a known latent-factor quantity without added utility;
- technical choices dominate conclusions;
- a simpler established method supplies the same decision more reliably;
- TCGA-only behavior does not reproduce externally;
- no prospectively defined scientific task benefits from the added complexity.

## 11. Success criterion

Success is not recovery of old GRI.

Success is a reproducible method that helps researchers decide, decompose, predict, stratify, or design experiments better than an appropriate baseline, while preserving explicit uncertainty, traceability, and refusal states.

The program is free to abandon every historical GRI equation if that is what the evidence requires.
