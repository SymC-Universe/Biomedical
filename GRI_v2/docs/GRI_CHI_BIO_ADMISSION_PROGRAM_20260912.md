# GRI Chi_bio admission-or-falsification program

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** P0-D/P0-Q PROGRAM SPECIFICATION, NOT AN ADMITTED BIOLOGICAL COORDINATE  
**Protocol authority:** General Cross-Project Research Protocol Final v0.7.4  
**Notation rule:** `Chi_bio` denotes the candidate **system-level biological Chi** investigated here. It is distinct from the lower-level candidate dynamical response coordinate `chi_dyn = Gamma/(2|Omega|)` already governed by `CHI_ADMISSION_RULES.md`.

## 1. Purpose

The central question is now:

> Can the scalar, modal/vector, and conglomerate organization already resolved in GRI support a reproducible, independently derived system-level biological coordinate `Chi_bio`, and if so does unity have a natural and independently testable boundary meaning?

This program is explicitly an **admission-or-falsification** program. It does not assume that a coherent scalar `Chi_bio` exists. Acceptable outcomes include:

- a derivable and identifiable `Chi_bio`;
- a derivable but currently non-identifiable `Chi_bio` requiring specific new observables;
- a regime-conditional or representation-conditional `Chi_bio`;
- a mode-resolved family that cannot be compressed coherently to one system scalar;
- no scientifically useful system-level `Chi_bio` under the tested models.

## 3. Semantic contract

If ultimately admitted, `Chi_bio` is intended to be a **dimensionless system-level compressed coordinate of regulatory balance/stability architecture** whose meaning is fixed by its governing System Model and derivation.

It is not defined as:

- `CV/2`;
- `S_spec`;
- H2/global CKA;
- H3a patient-specific coupling;
- H3b same-label semantic specificity;
- principal-angle alignment;
- an arithmetic or fitted weighted average of scalar, modal, and conglomerate metrics;
- a score chosen because it places cancers attractively around 1;
- a substitute for the modal/vector or conglomerate views.

The scalar/modal/conglomeration firewall remains controlling. A useful `Chi_bio` may compress information, but disagreement among the three views must remain observable and may itself force refusal of a scalar description.

## 4. Candidate construction rule

Candidate forms must be derived from a biologically and mathematically stated System Model before cancer positions relative to unity are used for model selection.

Allowed P0-D candidate-generator classes include, without selecting among them yet:

1. linearized regulatory state-space/Jacobian models;
2. coupled-mode or generalized-eigenvalue models;
3. second-order response models where biology genuinely supplies that order;
4. delay/memory/feedback models;
5. transition-operator or cell-state-flow models;
6. perturbation-response/recovery operators;
7. other native oncology/regulatory generators that yield a dimensionless balance coordinate by derivation.

A candidate generator is not privileged because it resembles prior SymC mathematics. The native biological model must license the construction.

## 5. Hard anti-fitting firewalls

The following invalidate a candidate for confirmatory use:

- selecting the formula after inspecting which construction places cancers closest to `Chi_bio = 1`;
- selecting numerator/denominator terms because they make a preferred cancer cross unity;
- choosing weights from downstream clinical labels or Atlas regions later used as validation;
- defining unity by median-centering or other post-result rescaling and then calling 1 a biological boundary;
- using the same decisive outcome both to construct and validate the coordinate;
- borrowing certainty from modal or conglomerate evidence to repair a non-identifiable scalar;
- suppressing cancers or modes because they disagree with the preferred scalar story.

## 6. Chi_bio gates

### CB1 - semantic definition
The intended meaning, scope, non-claims, and unit/normalization class are frozen before outcome interpretation.

### CB2 - generator and derivation
A native biological/mathematical generator is stated and the candidate coordinate follows from it. A formula may not be chosen solely by empirical fit.

### CB3 - dimensionless or otherwise justified normalization
The coordinate is dimensionless by derivation or has a prospectively justified normalization that gives comparisons a coherent meaning.

### CB4 - independently meaningful inputs
All input observables are meaningful before being combined. Algebraic reconstruction of one decisive input from another is disclosed and cannot masquerade as independent information.

### CB5 - scalar/modal/conglomerate relationship
The derivation states what information each view contributes, what is discarded by compression, and when disagreement forces refusal. No omnibus average is permitted merely for convenience.

### CB6 - outcome and Atlas independence
Coordinate construction does not use the downstream biological outcome, Atlas placement, favored region, or desired unity crossing that will later be presented as evidence.

### CB7 - identifiability
The candidate is distinguishable, within a stated validity regime and uncertainty, from materially different generators or parameterizations capable of producing the same observed data.

### CB8 - uncertainty and tolerance
Input uncertainty, estimation uncertainty, and model/representation uncertainty are propagated. Boundary/regime labels are withheld where uncertainty spans the relevant threshold.

### CB9 - construction-aware nulls and known-truth recovery
Synthetic/negative controls demonstrate that the implementation can recover the intended coordinate when it exists and refuses or fails in known non-identifiable/non-applicable cases.

### CB10 - internal P0-Q robustness
The frozen candidate survives the planned internal TCGA qualification suite without post-result retuning, or its validity regime is narrowed transparently.

### CB11 - refusal and validity regime
Explicit conditions are defined for `NO_COHERENT_CHI_BIO`, `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`, `OUT_OF_VALIDITY_REGIME`, `UNCERTAINTY_SPANS_BOUNDARY`, and representation-dependent refusal.

### CB12 - pre-Atlas freeze
The candidate formula, implementation, normalization, uncertainty model, and refusal rules are frozen before decisive Atlas/boundary interpretation.

### CB13 - untouched external transport/validation
A separately frozen P1 test on independent evidence evaluates the candidate without redefinition or favorable source selection based on GRI agreement.

### CB14 - unity-boundary license
`Chi_bio = 1` receives biological boundary meaning only if unity is generated naturally by the derivation/normalization **and** a separately defined biological regime distinction changes at or consistently around that boundary on evidence not used to create it. A freely estimated alternative threshold must remain visible as a comparator where scientifically appropriate.

## 7. Promotion states

### State A - NOT_ADMITTED
Current state. No `Chi_bio` value or biological unity-boundary plot is allowed.

### State B - CANDIDATE_LOCKED
Requires CB1-CB9 plus frozen candidate code/configuration. This locks a definition for testing but does not validate it biologically.

### State C - INTERNALLY_QUALIFIED_P0Q
Requires State B + CB10-CB11. This may support an internally qualified system coordinate within the tested TCGA representation. It is not external confirmation and does not license temporal/dynamical meaning.

### State D - EXTERNALLY_ADMITTED_P1
Requires State C + CB12-CB13. This licenses the coordinate only for the externally tested validity regime and claim ceiling.

### State E - UNITY_BOUNDARY_ADMITTED
Requires State D + CB14. Only this state licenses a plot or statement in which `Chi_bio = 1` is presented as an empirically supported biological boundary rather than a candidate/reference line.

## 11. External-data role

Static external cohorts can test coordinate transport if their representation can be harmonized independently. Longitudinal/perturbational cohorts are required for any later claim that `Chi_bio` represents recovery, resilience, transition dynamics, or a temporal stability regime.

Current source-qualified candidates remain P0-D and outcome-unopened. Selection for decisive P1 use must be frozen independently of GRI agreement.

## 12. Current stop boundary

Safe work can proceed through terminology separation, machine-readable gate definition, dependency inventory, synthetic/null design, Atlas guardrails, source qualification, and exact packaging specification.

The first true science-changing stop occurs when one or more candidate generators survive enough scrutiny that choosing which candidate(s) to freeze for C5 could change the scientific definition of `Chi_bio`. That selection requires explicit scientific review before outcome-bearing `Chi_bio` values are computed or unity is inspected.
