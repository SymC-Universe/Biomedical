## 1. Scientific choices now frozen

The approved candidate-development path is:

1. **Primary generator family:** G1 normalized regulatory-interaction operator.
2. **Biological state semantics:** S1 transcriptomic regulatory state.
3. **Methylation role:** substrate/context modifier of the regulatory map, not a naively concatenated component of the state vector.
4. **System scope:** L3 paired local + embedded analysis.
5. **Temporal comparator:** G2 discrete transition operator.
6. **Stochastic alternative:** G4 attraction/diffusion family retained as a materially different competing explanation.

These choices were made before cancer-specific Chi_bio values or Atlas/unity placement were inspected.

## 2. Frozen G1 source-model form

For local transcriptomic regulatory state `x`, substrate/context `s` and other frozen or measured context `c`, the candidate source-model class is

```text
dx/dt = beta0 * (Phi(x; s, c) - x),  beta0 > 0.
```

At a fixed point `x*`, define the normalized regulatory-interaction operator

```text
M_GRI = D_x Phi(x*; s, c).
```

The corresponding local Jacobian is

```text
J_local = beta0 * (M_GRI - I).
```

Therefore

```text
max Re eig(J_local) = beta0 * (max Re eig(M_GRI) - 1).
```

Within this source-model class only, the candidate scalar is

```text
G1 = max Re eig(M_GRI).
```

and the mathematical local-stability partition is

```text
G1 < 1  -> locally asymptotically stable linearization
G1 = 1  -> local spectral boundary
G1 > 1  -> locally unstable linearization
```

This unity boundary is generated by the restoring identity term of the model. It is not introduced by median centering, outcome fitting, Atlas placement, or post-hoc rescaling.

## 3. Scalar / modal / conglomerate relation

The three views are frozen as different projections of one operator, not as ingredients of a weighted score:

- **conglomerate/system object:** `M_GRI` together with its substrate/context embedding;
- **modal/vector view:** eigenvalues, left/right eigenvectors or invariant subspaces, non-normality diagnostics and mode participation;
- **scalar candidate view:** the leading real-part eigenvalue `max Re eig(M_GRI)` when the representation is identifiable and the scalar compression is licensed.

No arithmetic averaging of lower-level GRI coordinates is permitted to create Chi_bio.

## 4. Local versus embedded meaning

The approved L3 scope requires two distinct objects where data permit:

- **local/intrinsic:** regulatory operator conditional on the modeled substrate/context;
- **embedded/realized:** observed system behavior after composition, microenvironment, treatment and other external coupling are represented.

A local G1 value may not be relabeled as a bulk-tumor system value without an explicit hierarchical-closure or transport argument. Failure of local-to-embedded preservation is an admissible negative result.

## 5. What methylation is allowed to do

Methylation/substrate information may:

- parameterize `Phi`;
- change regulatory coefficients or sensitivities;
- define substrate-conditioned operator families;
- enter an explicit coupled context model;
- be used to test substrate inheritance or its failure.

It may not be concatenated with RNA merely to preserve all available information, and it may not be used to tune the candidate toward unity.

## 6. What is deliberately not frozen yet

The following remain open because choosing them would create a more specific empirical estimator:

- exact transcriptomic dimensional reduction;
- exact regulatory-map estimator;
- regularization family and tuning rule;
- how substrate/context parameters enter the empirical map;
- local-to-embedded closure model;
- uncertainty estimator for the empirical operator;
- exact refusal thresholds for non-identifiability or representation instability;
- external P1 cohort and comparator freeze.

These must be selected prospectively and outcome-blind before real cancer-specific Chi_bio values are computed.

## 7. Immediate qualification sequence

The candidate family may now proceed through safe pre-outcome work:

1. complete the candidate-level MFR-14 record;
2. freeze generator-specific synthetic known-truth and known-failure cases;
3. test G1 mathematical recovery, unity classification and Jacobian equivalence;
4. test non-normal and defective/near-defective cases where scalar compression may be insufficient;
5. compare against G2 and G4 at the identifiability level;
6. design outcome-blind empirical state-reduction candidates;
7. stop again before selecting an empirical reduction if multiple scientifically material choices survive.

## 8. Claim ceiling

This freeze establishes **what candidate family is being tested**. It does not establish that a biological Chi exists, that TCGA identifies `M_GRI`, that any cancer has a particular Chi_bio value, or that `Chi_bio = 1` is a biological regime boundary.

`Chi_bio` remains `NOT_ADMITTED` until the downstream admission gates are actually earned.
