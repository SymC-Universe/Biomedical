## Purpose

This record does not duplicate the prior v0.7.1A migration audit. It identifies only the additional controls introduced or hardened by Final v0.7.4 and maps them onto the current GRI state after completion of post-C1 adversarial sensitivity v2.2.

Final v0.7.4 is prospective. It does not convert existing TCGA evidence into temporal, causal, mechanistic, hierarchical, or confirmatory evidence and does not retroactively promote prior exploratory results.

## 1. Immediate v0.7.4 impact on GRI

The current GRI architecture remains scientifically viable under v0.7.4, but the next stage must be framed more explicitly as a **relational and hierarchical state-space investigation**, not merely a static cross-omic alignment analysis.

The new controls do not invalidate Stage C1. They change what must be separated before future higher-level claims are admitted:

- local regulatory structure versus embedded realized tumor behavior;
- coordinate construction versus Atlas interpretation;
- participation versus observability versus identifiability versus estimation uncertainty;
- lower-scale representation versus validated higher-scale closure;
- state classification versus perturbation recovery/resilience;
- recurring mathematical organization versus common biological mechanism;
- experimental opportunity versus literature-shaped question selection;
- inherited prior art versus residual GRI contribution.

## 2. Section 21.2: local dynamical identity versus embedded realized behavior

**Current status: PARTIAL / NOT YET DIRECTLY TESTED.**

Existing GRI evidence describes static cross-layer organization among methylation, RNA, composition covariates, and related modalities. It does not demonstrate that a locally inferred regulatory object determines the behavior realized after embedding in tumor-cell, microenvironmental, pathway, spatial, and treatment context.

Current rule:

`LOCAL REGULATORY ORGANIZATION != EMBEDDED REALIZED TUMOR BEHAVIOR`

The Function Map may report cross-layer relationships, but any future embedded-stability claim requires an explicit representation of coupling/environment/feedback or an appropriately measured longitudinal/perturbational analogue.

No system-level scalar may be inferred from lower-level scalar/proxy coordinates merely by aggregation.

## 3. Section 21.3: pre-Atlas coordinate independence

**Current status: ARCHITECTURALLY SUPPORTED; FUTURE P1 MUST RE-AUDIT.**

The existing GRI development path already separates molecular coordinate construction from future independent Atlas interpretation. Current C1/H2/H3 calculations were not defined by external Atlas labels or a desired Atlas region.

Prospective rule is now explicit:

`native observables -> System Model/Engine coordinates -> coupling/system organization -> independent Atlas interpretation`

Future Atlas labels, disease states, outcomes, favored regions, or agreement with GRI may not define, tune, select, or transform the coordinate later interpreted by the Atlas.

This becomes a required P1 independence check before any decisive external Atlas use.

## 4. Sections 21.4-21.5: participation, observability, identifiability, coordinate estimation, regime placement

**Current status: PARTIAL / OUTPUT SCHEMA NEEDS FUTURE EXTENSION.**

The current GRI control plane already exposes uncertainty, modal output, cross-component structure, admission/refusal, and identifiability-oriented concepts, but it does not yet make all v0.7.4 distinctions explicit in one mature output record.

Before production Tool freeze, outputs should separate where scientifically meaningful:

- object admitted in System Model;
- participation/contribution to the observed response;
- observability from available molecular measurements;
- identifiability relative to competing structures;
- coordinate estimate;
- uncertainty/tolerance;
- regime/branch placement if a licensed boundary exists;
- whether placement is resolved.

For current GRI specifically, no biological chi or physical under/critical/overdamped branch is admitted. Therefore regime placement for a biological chi is `NOT_APPLICABLE`/`NOT_ADMITTED`, not a missing implementation to be filled.

## 5. Section 21.6: hierarchical closure

**Current status: NOT ESTABLISHED.**

Static cross-layer alignment is not hierarchical closure.

No present result shows that a lower-scale regulatory representation preserves the quantities required for a higher-scale tumor-behavior question under a tested validity regime.

Future hierarchical claims must define the higher-level quantity that is supposed to survive reduction. Candidate preservation targets may include, only where native oncology science licenses them:

- perturbation response;
- state transition ordering;
- treatment-response trajectory;
- recovery/reorganization timescale;
- uncertainty relevant to a higher-level decision;
- cross-modal latent organization that can be independently measured at both levels.

Current status should remain `UNRESOLVED` until such a test exists.

## 6. Section 21.7 and prediction S5: recovery/resilience

**Current status: NOT TESTABLE FROM STATIC TCGA.**

TCGA cross-sectional ordering cannot establish recovery, resilience erosion, reorganization after disturbance, or transient amplification.

The newly source-qualified longitudinal/perturbational candidates are therefore scientifically important because they may support future P0-D/P0-Q recovery questions, but they do not inherit a recovery claim from static C1.

Potential future distinction:

`CURRENT STATE != PERTURBATION RESPONSE != RECOVERY/REORGANIZATION`

No S5 claim is currently activated.

## 7. Section 21.8 and prediction S6: cross-scale/hierarchical preservation

**Current status: NOT ACTIVATED AS CONFIRMATORY CLAIM.**

Repeated cross-omic or cross-cancer geometry is not evidence that the same biological mechanism operates across scales, nor that one numerical chi region governs them.

Future work must distinguish:

1. recurring mathematical/statistical motif;
2. preserved architecture across grouping or scale change;
3. common mechanism;
4. common numerical chi region.

Current GRI evidence can support the first category only where explicitly demonstrated. Categories 2-4 require separate evidence.

## 8. Section 50.2: Experimental Opportunity and Literature Collision Pass

**Current status: PROSPECTIVE CONTROL ADDED; NO RETROACTIVE PRE-SEARCH CLAIM.**

External-source and prior-literature discovery was already underway before v0.7.4. The program must not relabel literature-informed questions as independently ideated pre-search experiments.

For new substantial GRI questions after 2026-09-12:

1. baseline native oncology/genomics orientation;
2. preserve an independent experiment/perturbation game plan for the residual question;
3. only then perform the targeted prior-experiment collision search for that same question;
4. classify what is already answered, partially answered, method-available, conflicting, or genuinely residual;
5. decide whether any physical/biological experiment should actually be built or whether existing evidence is the better answer.

Human, animal, clinical, pathogen, hazardous-chemistry, or regulated work remains outside unsupervised execution.

The most immediate GRI use of this control is not fabrication. It is to define future perturbational/longitudinal questions cleanly enough that existing experiments can be searched as potential known-truth tests before new wet-lab expenditure is considered.

## 9. Section 50.3: attribution and residual novelty

**Current status: PARTIAL; FORMAL RESIDUAL-NOVELTY RECORD STILL NEEDED BEFORE MAJOR NOVELTY CLAIM.**

The existing native-comparator survey and external-source audits already reduce novelty inflation by identifying established integration, prediction, and latent-factor methods.

Before a major GRI novelty claim is promoted, preserve a residual-novelty record with:

- `ESTABLISHED_BEFORE_THIS_WORK`
- `METHODS_OR_IDEAS_INHERITED_FROM_PRIOR_WORK`
- `KNOWN_RESULTS_USED_AS_CALIBRATION`
- `CLOSEST_PRIOR_ART`
- `RESIDUAL_UNANSWERED_QUESTION`
- `CANDIDATE_NEW_CONTRIBUTION`
- `NOT_CLAIMED_AS_NEW`

Likely candidate residual contribution remains the integrated audit/refusal/state-architecture workflow rather than ownership of CKA, methylation-expression association, latent-factor analysis, ridge prediction, or ordinary cross-omic integration primitives. Final wording remains contingent on the nearest-prior-art audit.

## 10. Sections 25-28: run economy, retry, checkpoint, supersession, AI continuity

**Current status: RECENT LOCAL EXECUTION EXPOSED AND CORRECTED A REAL GAP.**

The first v2.2 sensitivity launch exposed deterministic packaging/launcher defects before scientific computation:

- missing Python package root;
- ambiguous/wrong completed-C1 state handoff;
- absence of a complete preflight that rejected the sensitivity folder before worker launch.

The v2.2A launcher repair added complete path preflight and explicit completed-C1 folder selection without changing scientific worker bytes. The successful production run then verified the correct old state before scale.

Future expensive GRI runs should therefore preserve:

- exact input identities;
- semantic identity types;
- end-to-end production-path preflight;
- one representative production-equivalent case before scale;
- checkpoint unit/resume key;
- deterministic versus transient retry classification;
- stale-execution/supersession key where asynchronous execution is used;
- durable external AI work-state for long assistant-driven workflows.

This is now an evidenced operational requirement, not hypothetical bureaucracy.

## 11. Section 36.3: milestone scientific and governance balance

### Function versus limit balance

Post-C1 v2.2 materially strengthens the Function Map while retaining real limits:

Supported internal function/architecture now includes repeated-null H2/H3a survival, complete-case robustness, promoter-core robustness, residual geometry after leading-mode ablation, and gated composition-projected persistence.

Limits remain visible:

- H3b is weak/support-sensitive;
- S2 missingness projection is broadly unevaluable because 27/32 cancers lack variation in one or both burdens;
- composition projection is not comprehensive composition adjustment;
- static TCGA cannot establish temporal recovery, resilience, causality, or hierarchical closure;
- independent external Atlas/P1 confirmation remains absent.

### Coverage balance consequence

The project should **not** respond to this result with another long sequence of increasingly technical internal TCGA attacks unless a specific unresolved contradiction demands one.

The next scientific emphasis should move toward:

- representative external function;
- genuine biological perturbation/ordered-state evidence;
- meaningful transition/recovery architecture where available;
- independently selected confirmation;
- comparator and residual-novelty closure.

This is the v0.7.4 balance correction.

### Governance-burden consequence

Existing v0.7.1A control artifacts should be extended only where v0.7.4 adds a materially useful distinction. Do not duplicate every prior procedure under a new dated filename. The current authority file plus this delta audit are sufficient control documents unless executable semantics genuinely require a code/schema change.
