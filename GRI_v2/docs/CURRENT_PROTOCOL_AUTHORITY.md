The GP now intentionally retains program-wide transferable rules while project-specific equations, dataset conventions, atlas targets, implementation details, comparator candidates, and domain-specific examples remain local to the GRI project unless they are required to execute a general rule safely.

- the exact GRI proxy/coordinate definitions and their epistemic classes;
