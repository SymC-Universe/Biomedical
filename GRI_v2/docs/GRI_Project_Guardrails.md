# GRI Project Guardrails

Status: Active internal project safeguard
Program manual: SymC General Operations Manual v0.8.0
Purpose: preserve project-specific claim ceilings that were intentionally removed from the program-wide manual during consolidation.

## 1. Transcriptomic chi remains an operational proxy unless mechanistically licensed

For GRI, the observable quantity `chi_RNA = sigma/(2 mu) = CV/2` is an operational fluctuation-to-signal or stability proxy unless and until a validated mechanistic derivation establishes that the RNA-seq observables used actually identify the dynamical parameters required by the claimed `gamma/(2 omega)` construction in the relevant biological system.

Algebraic resemblance to the canonical damping-ratio form does not by itself license a formal EP2, critical-damping, or exceptional-point interpretation. A stronger mechanistic mapping must enter the ordinary GOM promotion path and cannot retroactively upgrade earlier proxy evidence.

## 2. Native count-statistical structure must be challenged

Any use of `sigma/(2 mu)` must explicitly test material dependence on the native RNA-seq sampling model and measurement process, including mean-variance coupling, sequencing depth, normalization, batch/composition effects, censoring/zero structure, and other relevant nuisance structure. A Poisson-only null is not sufficient when a negative-binomial or other overdispersed count model is the more appropriate native baseline.

## 3. Cross-sectional order is not time

Cross-sectional TCGA ordering, rank, severity geometry, or pseudotrajectory cannot by itself establish temporal progression, warning lead time, recovery, irreversibility, treatment response, or causal direction. Temporal claims require longitudinal evidence or a separately validated temporal model under GOM Section 20.4.

## 4. Manuscript-language reconciliation gate

Any manuscript, figure, supplement, repository description, or framework note that presents `chi_RNA = sigma/(2 mu)` as directly derived from the formal EP2 discriminant boundary, or as a mechanistically identified damping ratio rather than an operational proxy, is scientifically unresolved until one of two paths is completed:

1. the language is revised to the proxy/operational claim actually supported; or
2. the required mechanistic derivation and native validation are supplied and survive ordinary GOM qualification and promotion.

This guardrail does not pre-decide which path will succeed. It prevents the stronger claim from being inherited silently while the derivation remains unresolved.

## 5. Scope

These rules are GRI-specific implementation constraints. The transferable program-wide principles remain in GOM Sections 19.1, 20.4, and 40. They belong here because the exact RNA observable, statistical confounders, and manuscript claim ceiling are project-local rather than program-wide.
