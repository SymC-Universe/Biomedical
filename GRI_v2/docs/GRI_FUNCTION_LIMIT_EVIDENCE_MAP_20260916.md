# GRI Function Map + Limit Map + evidence-independence map

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Epistemic scope:** internal P0-D/P0-Q unless a row explicitly states otherwise  
**Purpose:** keep functioning regions, failures/refusals, and evidence dependence visible together instead of allowing a manuscript narrative to show only favorable structure.

## Reading rule

A Function-Map entry describes where a representation or relationship is supported under its stated construction. A Limit-Map entry describes where that support fails, becomes non-identifiable, becomes representation-dependent, or is not licensed. Neither map erases the other.

This document is a reconciliation layer. Exact numerical claims remain subordinate to their frozen result artifacts.

## A. Static Pan-Cancer architecture

### Function Map

**RNA architecture.** The frozen static program supports reproducible Hallmark-scale RNA organization after the declared sample-size calibration. This is a static organizational result.

**Composition/context.** Measured composition explains a concentrated portion of the RNA geometry, especially in expected immune/inflammatory sectors, while broader structure remains after the declared context analysis.

**Genomic context.** Admitted genomic burden coordinates contribute distributed context but do not replace the broader transcriptomic architecture with a single genomic organizer under the tested construction.

**Protein/phosphoprotein coupling.** Patient-aligned RNA organization retains detectable cross-assay information in the orthogonal protein/phosphoprotein layer under its declared null construction.

**Methylation/substrate architecture.** Genome-wide methylation supplies a distinct regulatory/substrate layer. Static methylation/RNA organization, patient-specific coupling, modal behavior, and context sensitivity can be analyzed without declaring methylation and RNA to be one state variable.

### Limit Map

- Static TCGA does not measure recovery, relaxation rate, temporal transition probability, or causal direction.
- Cross-sectional organization does not license damping, exceptional-point, or biological critical-boundary language.
- `chi_RNA = sigma/(2 mu) = CV/2` remains an operational historical proxy, not an admitted biological chi.
- Earlier Poisson-style or mean-variance arguments do not by themselves turn `CV/2` into a mechanistic dynamical coordinate.
- Composition adjustment is bounded by what was measured and cannot be described as complete microenvironment removal.
- Cross-assay association is not causal regulatory direction.

### Evidence-independence map

- TCGA RNA, methylation, genomic, composition, and RPPA layers belong to the same broad Pan-Cancer development program and are not automatically mutually confirmatory.
- Construction-aware nulls and orthogonal assays can strengthen internal qualification while remaining P0-Q unless an untouched external test is prospectively frozen.

## B. Legacy predictive internal program

### Function Map

The historical internal held-out program showed methylation-based predictive information beyond the declared purity/leukocyte covariates across most primary-evaluable cancers. It also produced an internal geometry/confidence relationship with prediction risk.

### Limit Map

- The comparison does not establish capacity-matched superiority to every reasonable Hallmark or latent baseline.
- It is internal TCGA evidence, not external clinical validation.
- Predictive performance does not establish causal methylation-to-RNA regulation.
- PCPG remains an informative post-result stress case with promotion debt, not untouched confirmation.

### Evidence-independence map

Discovery, internal replication, holdout, and later adversarial work share broader TCGA lineage and must retain their actual evidence class. Internal holdout is valuable but is not equivalent to an untouched external biological cohort.

### Function Map

**Chronic weekly experiment.** The frozen low-dimensional transition representation supports a bounded chronic result: predictive adequacy is present under the primary gate, the tested spectral-radius sensitivity envelopes are below the mathematical discrete-time unit circle at both frozen ranks, and the treatment-specific D2 operator was not supported under the declared analysis.

### Limit Map

**Short-term experiment.** Predictive adequacy failed at both frozen ranks. The sensitivity envelope crossed the mathematical unit circle, and the frozen escalation rule prevented opening D2 post hoc.

**Chronic representation dependence.** Warning behavior is not fully rank-independent, and the strict feature-gate challenge weakens D1 adequacy at `r=3`. The machine disposition remains `REPRESENTATION_DEPENDENT_NO_TRANSFER` where the declared material categories disagree.

**Interpretive firewall.** The discrete-time unit circle is a mathematical operator boundary. It is not, by itself, a biological `Chi_bio = 1` boundary, a recovery threshold, or a treatment-response threshold.

### Evidence-independence map

The SCC25 expression data have already been opened and used in G2 development/qualification. Any later state construction using the same expression may be useful P0-D/P0-Q evidence but cannot be represented as untouched P1 confirmation.

## E. G1 restoration/normalization lane

### Function Map

The theoretical G1 family supplies a native mathematical reason a unity boundary can arise in the special normalized interaction/restoration model class. Existing G2 temporal results constrain an effective transition operator and, where a continuous embedding is admissible, an effective generator family.

This makes restoration identifiability a concrete inverse problem rather than a free normalization constant.

### Limit Map

- The effective generator combines represented regulatory, self, restoration/degradation, and other effects; it does not uniquely identify a genome-wide restoration operator.
- A common SCC25 restoration constant is not justified merely because it creates a convenient normalized G1.
- Condition-matched kinetic inference from the same SCC25 experiment, if technically possible, improves matching but does not create evidentiary independence.
- External transcript-half-life measurements can bound plausibility but cannot automatically substitute cell-line- and condition-specific restoration values.
- Normalized empirical G1 therefore remains withheld until the restoration family is narrow enough that the scientific conclusion is invariant within uncertainty.

### Evidence-independence map

Restoration evidence should be labeled by role:

- same-experiment kinetic inference: condition matched, evidence dependent;
- external RNA-stability measurements: more independent, condition/cell-line mismatched unless specifically matched;
- frozen G2 transition evidence: constrains the combined generator but is not a separate restoration measurement.
