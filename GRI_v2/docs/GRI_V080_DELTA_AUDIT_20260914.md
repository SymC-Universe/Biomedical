# GRI v0.8.0 General-Protocol Delta Audit

**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Project:** GRI v2 / Chi_bio investigation  
**Program baseline:** General Cross-Project Research Protocol v0.8.0  
**Comparison available in this audit:** complete v0.7.7 -> v0.8.0 textual comparison  
**Immediate-predecessor caveat:** v0.8.0 declares `supersedes: 0.7.8`, but v0.7.8 was not available in the project repository or supplied comparison set at audit time. Therefore claims below about exact textual deltas are exact against v0.7.7, while immediate-predecessor claims remain unasserted until v0.7.8 is available.

## Executive finding

v0.8.0 is primarily a consolidation and transfer-of-responsibility revision rather than a scientific reset. The non-reducible scientific core remains active: MFR-14, native-model-first, chi epistemic classification, P0-D/P0-Q/P1/P2 separation, frozen-holdout discipline, refusal, Function Map + Limit Map, System Model -> Engine -> Independent Atlas architecture, relational stability, hierarchical closure, foundational-dependency robustness, reproducibility, standard-toolkit comparison, and bounded operational continuity.

The material GRI consequence is architectural: project-specific equations, dataset conventions, atlas targets, comparator candidates, implementation details, and domain examples no longer belong in the General Protocol merely because GRI was an originating use case. They must remain explicit in GRI-local authority documents.

No v0.8.0 change identified here justifies restarting, retuning, or reinterpreting an already frozen or already completed GRI calculation.

## GRI-specific material moved out of the GP

### Former GRI statistical/proxy content

The prior GRI-specific rule that the RNA coordinate is an operational proxy unless mechanistically derived, including concern about mean-variance structure and native count models, is now represented program-wide by Section 19.1: observational/statistical coordinates remain proxies until mechanistically licensed, and native sampling/confounding structure must be addressed.

**GRI action:** retain the exact GRI coordinate definitions, assay-specific models, transformations, and interpretation limits locally. Do not rely on the generalized GP to preserve GRI-specific equations.

### Former TCGA cross-sectional restriction

The previous GRI-specific cross-sectional warning is generalized in Section 19.2: cross-sectional order is not time.

**GRI action:** retain TCGA-specific and cohort-specific temporal/causal restrictions in the GRI project protocol and manuscript controls.

### Former GRI Regulatory Substrate Atlas section

The GP no longer carries the GRI Regulatory Substrate Atlas modalities and project population targets as program-wide text.

**GRI action:** keep the Regulatory Substrate Atlas schema, modality targets, evidence states, independence rules, and local provenance requirements in GRI-local documents. Existing atlas documents remain the project source of record until a consolidated GRI project protocol explicitly supersedes them.

### Former GRI chi-construction section

The project-specific list of regulatory/modal/substrate/conglomerate questions is no longer a GP domain section. The general construction architecture remains in Sections 2, 16, 20, and 21.

**GRI action:** preserve the exact GRI mapping from local observables to scalar, vector/modal, conglomerate/system, open-channel, inheritance, and candidate higher-order structure locally. This is especially important for the ongoing Chi_bio candidate lineage.

## Other important generalizations that affect GRI

### Finite precision and literature evidence

Former chemistry-heavy safeguards are generalized as Section 18. GRI literature-derived numerical references and locked comparator/atlas values therefore inherit the same FULL/ABSTRACT/SNIPPET/BLOCKED evidence discipline where applicable.

### Measurement structure and inference

Former neuro-specific safeguards become program-wide Section 20. GRI therefore explicitly inherits:

- no manufactured whole-system scalar;
- preservation of meaningful absence, spatial/hierarchical structure where relevant;
- level-specific identity and independence;
- separation of association, estimation, detection/classification, power, and prediction;
- baseline/reference locking only after qualification;
- strongest-simple-baseline and native-toolkit comparison for incremental-value claims.

### Foundational dependency

Section 11.3 retains the substantive robustness rule but simplifies the minimum dependency-record schema. This is an operational reduction, not permission to omit dependency consequences when they are material.

### Monitoring and execution continuity

Monitoring is consolidated into Sections 31.1-31.3 while retaining liveness-vs-progress separation, bounded recovery, circuit breaking, checkpoint identity, stale/superseded execution protection, known-bad watchdog qualification, and monitor-coverage audit.

For GRI, this supports continued progress without repeated PI prompts, while preserving the boundary that science-changing state definitions, thresholds, claims, or frozen commitments do not auto-advance.
