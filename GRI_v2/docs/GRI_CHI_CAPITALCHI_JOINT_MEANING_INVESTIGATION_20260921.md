## 2. Why weekly SCC25 is the first joint testbed

The published weekly SCC25 experiment contains:

- matched ordered RNA measurements;
- matched ordered DNA methylation measurements;
- cetuximab perturbation and time-matched PBS controls;
- measured proliferation/phenotypic response;
- 11-week progression into acquired resistance;

That makes it materially stronger for chi<->Chi joint meaning than cross-sectional TCGA.

The first joint object should therefore be a **partial SCC25 Chi architecture**, not the full TCGA eight-block carrier:

`Chi_SCC25(t) = {R(t), S(t), M(t), T(t), Q(t)}`

with proliferation retained as an external phenotype/validation observable rather than folded into the carrier.

## 4. Non-circularity firewall

No joint claim is earned merely because R/M features used to construct `T` are also present inside capital Chi.

The joint test must distinguish:

- shared construction;
- statistical association;
- incremental held-out information;
- mechanistic interpretation.

At least one challenge must use information not algebraically guaranteed by construction, such as methylation organization, independent phenotype, alternate held-out time transitions, or a separately constructed representation.

## 5. Comparator split by function

The source paper's CoGAPS analysis is the source-native comparator for evolving molecular-program description.

DMD/DMDc/reduced-rank state-space models are the native comparator family for transition/operator prediction.

Persistence and arm-specific mean-next-state remain simple forecast baselines.

No method is allowed to "win" by answering a different question.

## 7. Falsification outcomes

All are admissible:

- scalar sufficient, broader Chi adds no value;
- broader Chi adds information beyond scalar;
- scalar adds information beyond broader Chi;
- each carries different useful information;
- relationship is representation dependent;
- neither predicts adequately;
- no coherent scalar survives, while a multirepresentational architecture remains useful;
- neither scalar nor broader architecture transports.

The purpose is to learn the relationship, not to preserve a preferred hierarchy.
