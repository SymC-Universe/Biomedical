## 1. Definition used in this lineage

In this lineage, **Chi-bio means the capital-Chi biological stability architecture**, not a universal scalar `chi_bio`.

Capital Chi is the structured biological state formed by the measurable attributes, modal carriers, cross-layer couplings, embedding/context, temporal behavior where directly observed, uncertainty, and failure/refusal structure of the system.

Lowercase scalar chi is optional. A damped-oscillator ratio is one possible model-dependent realization only when a native biological model earns it. Neither scalarity nor damped oscillation is required by this program.

The development target is therefore not:

`find one biological number`

and not:

`force cancer into a damped oscillator`.

The development target is:

`construct a reproducible, interpretable capital-Chi carrier that can support diagnostic and predictive heads, then test whether it adds useful information beyond simpler baselines.`

## 2. Tool finish line

The intended research tool has one shared, frozen architecture carrier and two separately validated functions:

1. **Diagnostic/current-state function:** characterize or classify an independently defined current biological state.
2. **Predictive function:** predict a prospectively defined future, perturbational, transition, progression, response, recovery, resistance, or other independently measured target.

The carrier may remain multivariate or multilayer. A scalar summary is allowed only if it emerges as a useful, identifiable compression and survives separate ablation and validation. A scalar is not required for tool success.

Clinical deployment is outside this v1 finish line. Clinical use requires its own future intended-use, threshold, transportability, and prospective clinical validation program.

## 3. Frozen capital-Chi carrier blocks

The v1 carrier preserves the following blocks as distinct objects. Missing blocks are recorded as missing or not applicable, never silently set to zero.

| Block | Meaning | Current ancestry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `R` | RNA regulatory organization | Stage A / Stage C1 static architecture |
| `S` | methylation / regulatory-substrate organization | Stage C1 and post-C1 |
| `E` | embedding/context | Stage B1 purity/leukocyte composition and future compatible context |
| `G` | genomic constraint/context | Stage B2 genomic coordinates |
| `P` | protein/phosphoprotein coupling | Stage B2 RPPA |
| `M` | modal/vector carrier structure | Stage A/C1 eigenspectra, principal-angle/mode structure, top-mode sensitivity |
| `T` | temporal/operator structure | ordered G2 evidence where directly measured |
| `Q` | quality, uncertainty, robustness, identifiability, confidence, and refusal metadata | P0 predictive/confidence program plus project refusal rules |

Cross-block relationships are part of the carrier. Examples include `S <-> R`, `E -> R/S interpretation`, `G <-> R`, `P <-> R`, local-versus-embedded relationships, and modal-versus-temporal relationships where the data support the comparison.

An untested edge remains `UNTESTED`. A failed edge remains visible. The carrier is not completed by inventing missing couplings.

## 4. Permanent anti-collapse rules

1. Do not add the eight blocks into a master score.
2. Do not average disagreement among scalar, modal/vector, and conglomerate views.
3. Do not treat a mathematical unit circle as a biological unity boundary.
4. Do not treat historical `CV/2` as biological damping or as capital Chi.
5. Do not infer time, recovery, resilience, or transition from static TCGA samples.
6. Do not treat a missing temporal block as temporal stability.
7. Do not let outcome labels select the carrier definition.
8. Do not let Atlas position select or tune the carrier.
9. Do not rescue a failed representation by changing dimension, threshold, regularization, feature family, or normalization after seeing the decisive outcome.
10. Do not describe a diagnostic or predictive capability as clinical utility before an appropriate external clinical gate is passed.

## 6. TCGA development firewall

The prior TCGA FINAL_HOLDOUT has already been opened for the existing predictive architecture. Therefore:

`TCGA is development/qualification evidence for Conglomerate Chi-bio v1, not untouched external confirmation of the new conglomerate carrier.`

The new carrier may use TCGA to:

- materialize and reconcile already-earned feature blocks;
- test leakage-safe transformations;
- perform internal ablations;
- compare block-preserving versus reduced representations;
- quantify missingness and overlap;
- estimate internal calibration and uncertainty;
- identify failure/refusal regions;
- benchmark against the retained simpler predictive core.

It may not use the previously opened final partition as if it were prospective evidence for a new post-result architecture.

## 7. Conglomerate materialization sequence

### C0. Source/readiness registry

Machine-audit every required upstream source, result identity, claim ceiling, and heavy-artifact dependency. This stage reads no new biological outcome.

### C1. Patient/system-level block materialization

Create a long-form carrier table with keys such as:

- `entity_id`
- `cohort_id`
- `cancer_or_system`
- `block_id`
- `feature_id`
- `value`
- `uncertainty`
- `missingness_status`
- `validity_regime`
- `evidence_class`
- `source_hash_or_run_id`

No cross-block aggregation is performed here.

### C2. Relationship layer

Materialize only prospectively declared, already-supported or separately tested relations among blocks. Untested relations remain absent/UNTESTED.

### C3. Internal conglomerate ablation

Compare, under a frozen development split/design:

- retained simpler predictive core;
- core + modal information;
- core + genomic context;
- core + RPPA/protein information;
- core + local/embedded/context information;
- static conglomerate carrier;
- temporal extension only in systems with direct ordered measurements.

Each added block must earn itself by prediction, calibration, robustness, uncertainty/refusal quality, or scientifically useful traceability. No block is retained merely for conceptual completeness.

### C4. Diagnostic-head freeze

Before opening the decisive labels, freeze:

- diagnostic target/reference standard;
- eligibility;
- comparator(s);
- metric(s);
- calibration/refusal rules;
- missingness handling;
- claim ceiling;
- MFR-14 consequences.

### C5. Predictive-head freeze

Separately freeze a genuinely forward/independent target such as perturbational response, transition, resistance, progression, recovery, recurrence, or another domain-native endpoint. Diagnostic labels do not automatically become predictive targets.

### C6. External validation

The final carrier/tool must be frozen before the selected external outcome is opened. External compatibility mapping is frozen from assay/source constraints, not from performance.

### C7. Tool and revision freeze

Close only when code, configs, data identities, tests, figures, result artifacts, uncertainty/refusal behavior, external validation, claim ledger, supplement, and reproducibility package resolve to one immutable version.

## 8. Required comparisons

The tool is not validated merely by finding structure. At minimum it must be compared against:

- the retained simpler internal predictive core where the same question applies;
- task-appropriate conventional baselines;
- an outcome-free reduced representation when appropriate;
- block ablations;
- missingness/technical/composition attacks relevant to the task;
- an always-predict or no-refusal baseline when refusal/confidence is claimed.

A conglomerate model that does not improve useful inference, calibration, robustness, or traceability is narrowed even if biologically interesting.

## 9. Current compute policy

The first authorized computation under this freeze is the **C0 Conglomerate Readiness Audit**. It is intentionally lightweight and GitHub-native. It checks whether each block is presently materializable from committed compact sources, requires an existing heavy artifact, requires source re-materialization, or requires genuinely new data.

The audit may not create a cancer ranking, fit a diagnostic endpoint, tune a new representation, or inspect an external confirmatory outcome.

Heavy C1/C2 materialization may move to Kaggle or another suitable compute environment after C0 identifies the exact required inputs. The scientific contract is environment-independent.

## 10. Success states

The v1 program can close in more than one scientifically valid way:

- `CONGLOMERATE_TOOL_VALIDATED`: the block-preserving carrier improves a frozen diagnostic/predictive task externally.
- `NARROWER_TOOL_VALIDATED`: a subset of blocks outperforms the full carrier and becomes the tool.
- `ARCHITECTURE_DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY`: reproducible architecture exists but does not improve prediction/diagnosis over fair baselines.
- `NO_PORTABLE_TOOL`: internal structure does not survive external validation.
- `REFUSED_NONIDENTIFIABLE`: carrier or task cannot be identified without unsupported assumptions.

Failure of one state does not authorize retuning the same external test.
