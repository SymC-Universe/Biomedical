# GRI GOM v0.8.0 continuity and source-of-record map

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Scope:** GRI oncology only  
**Program baseline:** General Cross-Project Research Protocol v0.8.0  
**Purpose:** preserve project-local scientific safeguards while making the current GRI evidence, manuscript, branch, and execution authorities unambiguous.

## 1. Program rule versus project science

GOM v0.8.0 supplies program-wide transferable controls. It does not replace GRI-specific equations, datasets, state definitions, thresholds, freezes, evidence roles, Atlas rules, or biological claim ceilings.

Accordingly:

- GOM controls epistemic class, MFR-14, promotion, refusal, independence, reproducibility, Function/Limit mapping, foundational dependency, monitoring, recovery, source-of-record behavior, and reader-facing synchronization.
- GRI project files control the oncology-native scientific object, datasets, representations, frozen analyses, thresholds, interpretation, and local refusal rules.
- A program consolidation may simplify duplicated governance text, but a GRI safeguard is not discarded merely because the GOM becomes more concise. A removed safeguard must have an explicit surviving local destination or be formally retired for a documented reason.

## 4. GOM v0.8.0 migration-integrity check

The following GRI safeguards must survive the migration regardless of where the wording lives:

1. historical `CV/2` cannot silently regain biological-chi status;
2. static cross-sectional evidence cannot inherit temporal, recovery, damping, or causal meaning;
3. scalar, modal/vector, and conglomerate/system representations remain complementary and non-arithmetic by default;
4. local dynamical identity and embedded realized behavior remain distinct;
5. the Independent Atlas may not define or rescue `Chi_bio`;
6. P0-D/P0-Q evidence cannot be relabeled P1 because a later interpretation is attractive;
7. already-opened SCC25 expression cannot become untouched external confirmation for later SCC25-derived representation work;
8. failed B3 compact-identifiability tests remain failures and cannot be tuned away;
9. `Chi_bio = 1` remains unclaimed unless a native derivation plus independent biological regime evidence earns it;
10. every production representation must retain refusal/indeterminate states;
11. favorable and unfavorable evidence remain visible in Function and Limit maps;
12. a post-result hypothesis carries promotion debt rather than retrospective prospective status.

## 5. Capability/prose synchronization

Whenever a computational or scientific capability changes, update the descriptions that materially depend on it. For GRI that currently means synchronizing, as needed:

- the living manuscript;
- manuscript reconciliation ledger;
- current dated status;
- PR #4 description;
- public README only when the change is mature enough for a public-facing pointer;
- figure captions and supplementary descriptions at final reconciliation.

Fresh prose is not evidence. It must point to the frozen result, code, or source audit that supports it.

## 6. What may be edited autonomously

Safe mechanical or governance edits include source-of-record maps, stale-liveness corrections, evidence-linking, manuscript placeholders, claim-ceiling synchronization, deterministic provenance validation, reproduction instructions, figure specifications, and reconciliation ledgers.

These edits may summarize existing results but may not change a frozen threshold, representation, biological state definition, evidentiary role, admission decision, or interpretation solely to improve narrative coherence.

## 7. What must stop for scientific review

Stop before:

- selecting a replacement biological state representation because B3 compact reduction failed;
- choosing a higher-dimensional or regularized operator after inspecting outcomes;
- replacing A3 rank refusal with a preferred rank;
- inserting a restoration constant or decay model for normalized G1 without a prospective rule;
- turning mathematical unit-circle behavior into a biological criticality claim;
- declaring an external candidate P1 when discovery/development evidence has already been opened;
- changing a project-local safeguard whose removal could alter a claim.
