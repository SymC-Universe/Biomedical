## 1. Why this lineage exists

The BioSystems tumor-normal control now supports a static result: tumor tissue shows a reproducible weakening of several within-layer/modular organization measures relative to TCGA adjacent normal tissue, while cross-layer organization persists and reorganizes heterogeneously. That result does not measure recovery and does not admit a biological scalar chi.

The historical scalar `Chi_bio` admission cycle remains closed as `NOT_ADMITTED`. This lineage does not reopen or retune it.

The new question is broader and recovery-first:

> How does biological organization before and during a perturbation constrain resistance, transient response, recovery, reorganization, memory, and failure to reclaim an admissible state, and can any local/modal scalar coordinate be licensed that adds information to the broader biological stability architecture?

The biological stability architecture is denoted here by capital `Χ_bio` only as the GOM representation label for the progressively reconstructed architecture. It is not presumed to be a scalar, a master score, or a complete ontology. Lowercase `χ_bio` is withheld unless a native dynamical model earns it.

## 2. Inherited facts and non-negotiable boundaries

### From GRI

Retain:

- static RNA, methylation, protein, genomic, and context architecture as separate evidence layers;
- the tumor-normal result as static specificity/control evidence;
- methylation/chromatin as a **structural-substrate candidate**, not a causal carrier;
- the SCC25 temporal lane as qualification/limit evidence only;
- the refused compact B3 state construction and unresolved G1 restoration identifiability as failures that may not be retuned away;
- no current biological chi, no biological unity boundary, no cancer optimum at chi=1.

Do not use the BioSystems revision as discovery evidence for a new recovery result except as declared hypothesis provenance.

### From Stability Inheritance

Use the current distinction:

- `SUBSTRATE_INFLUENCE`: parent/substrate perturbation changes a child observable;
- `CONDITIONAL_INHERITANCE`: a specified scalar, mode, subspace, or system structure is prospectively transmitted through a frozen mapping under stated conditions;
- `SUBSTRATE_INHERITANCE`: carrier-resolved parent architecture prospectively predicts the child, survives intervention, and defeats scrambled/generic controls.

A matched scalar alone is not inheritance. Carrier correspondence, intervention, specificity, and identifiability are separate gates.

### From ChemSA / generator-first work

Carry forward the methodological boundary, not a chemical mechanism:

- local scalar compression is licensed only after a governing model permits it;
- modal/vector structure and system organization must not be collapsed into a scalar by convenience;
- a general first-order biological generator does not automatically produce a mechanical damping ratio;
- poles/eigenmodes, response geometry, perturbation sensitivity, and refusal can be informative even when scalar chi is withheld.

## 3. Primary P0-D research questions

### Q1. Recovery architecture

For a defined perturbation-removal experiment, map separately:

1. pre-perturbation reference state/manifold/distribution;
2. perturbation identity, dose/amplitude, duration, and channel;
3. resistance/initial displacement;
4. transient amplification or overshoot;
5. first reclaim of a prospectively defined reference criterion;
6. sustained recovery;
7. recovery path and timescale;
8. final state: restoration, equivalent-state return, reorganization, persistent displacement, escape, or refusal;
9. hysteresis/path dependence;
10. repeated-perturbation or rechallenge behavior.

No default single resilience score is permitted.

### Q2. Joint lowercase-chi / capital-Chi meaning

If a local or modal scalar is licensed, test both directions of the relationship:

- Does the scalar/modal object predict resistance, recovery path, recovery time, reorganization, or repeated-perturbation behavior?
- Does broader system organization explain cases in which similar local scalar values produce different realized behavior?
- Can different internal organizations preserve the same external recovery phenotype?
- What information is lost by scalar compression?
- Does the broader architecture add held-out information beyond native biological baselines?
- If it does not, report that the simpler representation is sufficient for the declared question.

### Q3. Stability inheritance in biology

Test whether a pre-perturbation substrate layer, initially chromatin/methylation where data permit, carries information that survives into later response/recovery after controlling for baseline transcriptomic state and ordinary covariates.

The minimum evidence ladder is:

1. characterize the parent/substrate without looking at the target recovery result;
2. define carrier/subspace correspondence;
3. freeze a parent-to-child mapping;
4. prospectively predict an independently generated child/recovery object;
5. perturb the candidate substrate or use a justified counterfactual/intervention;
6. defeat scrambled-carrier, generic-state, expression-only, and other strong native/simple controls.

Static methylation-RNA association does not satisfy this ladder.

## 4. Native biological objects first

Candidate native observables include, depending on the testbed:

- cell-state transcriptome;
- chromatin accessibility / histone state / DNA methylation;
- signaling trajectories;
- proliferation, viability, apoptosis, or drug sensitivity;
- lineage/barcode identity;
- state-switching rates;
- return to a reference phenotype or transcriptional manifold;
- response under rechallenge.

Candidate dynamical representations to compare, not assume:

- state-space / hidden-state models;
- transition matrices or continuous-time generators;
- ODE/Jacobian models where experimentally supportable;
- dynamic-mode / Koopman-style decompositions;
- lineage-state Markov models;
- non-normal/transient-response analyses where justified;
- simple native recovery metrics and conventional predictive baselines.

No method is labeled SymC-specific merely because it uses eigenvalues, modes, state space, or recovery.

## 5. Scalar chi admission firewall

Lowercase `χ_bio` remains `WITHHELD` at opening.

A candidate scalar may be investigated only if:

1. the same biological coordinate/mode supplies compatible dynamical quantities;
2. the governing model licenses the reduction;
3. the quantities are independently identifiable;
4. uncertainty is bounded;
5. the construction is not normalized or tuned toward one;
6. representation/rank/basis dependence is either controlled or explicitly part of the scope;
7. the scalar adds held-out value beyond strong native/simple predictors for the same question;
8. any proposed boundary is derived from the model and frozen before decisive outcome inspection;
9. the interpretation transports to an independent system.

A complex eigenpair of an arbitrary first-order biological operator may be retained as pole/eigenmode geometry. It is not automatically promoted to a mechanical damping ratio.

## 7. First adversarial nulls

A candidate biological stability relation must survive, where applicable:

- baseline state alone;
- simple distance-to-reference alone;
- growth/viability or known resistance markers alone;
- expression-only prediction versus substrate-plus-expression;
- randomized carrier/subspace assignment;
- label-preserving patient/cell shuffle;
- time-order destruction;
- dose/time matched generic perturbation;
- dimensionality/capacity-matched baseline;
- alternate justified state representations;
- lineage leakage checks;
- batch/platform/context covariates;
- negative-control perturbations;
- no-washout datasets, which must refuse recovery claims.

## 8. First failure conditions

The investigation narrows rather than repairs itself if:

- no dataset supplies a defensible return target;
- recovery is not temporally distinguishable from survival under continuing treatment;
- pre-perturbation architecture adds no held-out value beyond native baselines;
- substrate information disappears after baseline state/context control;
- carrier mapping is non-identifiable;
- similar candidate scalars accompany materially different outcomes with no stable conditioning relation;
- no representation licenses a scalar;
- an apparent boundary depends on tuning, rank, basis, normalization, or post-result selection;
- independent transport fails.

A valid outcome is therefore a capital-Chi architecture with no admitted lowercase biological chi.
