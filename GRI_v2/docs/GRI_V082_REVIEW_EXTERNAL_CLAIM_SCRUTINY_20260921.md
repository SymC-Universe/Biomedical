## 1. Why this pass exists

Internal consistency, deterministic code, preregistration, and reproducible arithmetic are necessary but do not establish that a construct corresponds to biology. This pass therefore treats every material current GRI claim as a claim that can fail against domain knowledge, standard methods, prior experiments, measurement physics, or a better native explanation.

The questions are:

1. What exactly is the claim?
2. What native-domain fact has to be true for it to mean what we say it means?
3. What external evidence supports or challenges that prerequisite?
4. What might every internal investigator have inherited incorrectly?
5. What is the smallest discriminating test?
6. What survives if the challenge succeeds?
7. What must be narrowed if it fails?

## 3. Literature collision sources actually inspected

These sources are cited for the propositions above, not as blanket endorsements of GRI.

1. Love MI, Huber W, Anders S. **Moderated estimation of fold change and dispersion for RNA-seq data with DESeq2.** Genome Biology 15:550 (2014). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-014-0550-8  
   Relevant point: RNA-seq variance depends on mean and dispersion; `Var(K)=mu+alpha*mu^2`.

2. Aran D, Sirota M, Butte AJ. **Systematic pan-cancer analysis of tumour purity.** Nature Communications 6:8971 (2015). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms9971  
   Relevant point: tumour purity materially confounds correlation, clustering, and expression analysis in TCGA.

3. Spainhour JCG, Lim HS, Yi SV, Qiu P. **Correlation Patterns Between DNA Methylation and Gene Expression in The Cancer Genome Atlas.** Cancer Informatics 18 (2019). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/1176935119828776  
   Relevant point: methylation-expression relationships are context dependent and may have opposing signs even near one another.

4. Zhu B et al. **Integrating Clinical and Multiple Omics Data for Prognostic Assessment across Human Cancers.** Scientific Reports 7:16954 (2017). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-17031-8  
   Relevant point: TCGA multi-omic kernel similarity and kernel alignment are established prior art.

5. Kornblith S, Norouzi M, Lee H, Hinton G. **Similarity of Neural Network Representations Revisited.** ICML / PMLR 97 (2019). https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/kornblith19a.html  
   Relevant point: CKA is a representation-similarity measure, closely related to CCA.

6. Murphy AG, Zylberberg J, Fyshe A. **Correcting Biased Centered Kernel Alignment Measures in Biological and Artificial Neural Networks.** ICLR 2024 Representational Alignment Workshop / arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.01012  
   Relevant point: methodological challenge that biased CKA can inflate in low-n/high-p settings. This is challenge literature, not treated as a definitive peer-reviewed biological result.

7. Liberzon A et al. **The Molecular Signatures Database (MSigDB) hallmark gene set collection.** Cell Systems 1(6):417-425 (2015). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cels.2015.12.004  
   Relevant point: Hallmarks were built as coherent, reduced-redundancy gene sets; this supports use as a basis, not unique privilege.

8. Akbani R et al. **A pan-cancer proteomic perspective on The Cancer Genome Atlas.** Nature Communications 5:3887 (2014). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms4887  
   Relevant point: TCGA RPPA required replicate-based batch normalization and is a targeted antibody platform.

9. Stein-O'Brien G et al. **Integrated time course omics analysis distinguishes immediate therapeutic response from acquired resistance.** Genome Medicine 10:37 (2018). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1186/s13073-018-0545-2  
   Relevant point: exact SCC25 time-course source is truly ordered and perturbational; published native analysis used CoGAPS and found delayed methylation changes relative to transcription during resistance.

10. Rappoport N, Shamir R. **Multi-omic and multi-view clustering algorithms: review and cancer benchmark.** Nucleic Acids Research 46:10546-10562 (2018). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gky889  
    Relevant point: no integration method consistently dominated across benchmark criteria.

11. Cai Z, Poulos RC, Liu J, Zhong Q. **Machine learning for multi-omics data integration in cancer.** iScience 25:103798 (2022). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2022.103798  
    Relevant point: benchmarked early concatenation, PCA, MOFA2, DIABLO, iCluster and moCluster; simple methods can equal or outperform specialized integrators depending on task.

12. Singh A et al. **DIABLO: an integrative approach for identifying key molecular drivers from multi-omics assays.** Bioinformatics 35:3055-3062 (2019). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/bty1054  
    Relevant point: established supervised cross-omic latent integration for phenotype discrimination.

13. Flores JE et al. **Missing data in multi-omics integration: Recent advances through artificial intelligence.** Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence 6:1098308 (2023). DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2023.1098308  
    Relevant point: complete-case analysis is only safe under restrictive missingness assumptions; missing views require explicit handling.

14. Zack TI et al. **Pan-cancer patterns of somatic copy-number alteration.** Nature Genetics 45:1134-1140 (2013). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.2760  
    Relevant point: SCNAs are pervasive cancer genomic alterations with functional consequences, but are not themselves a stability metric.

## 4. New investigation target: scalar chi <-> capital Chi

The first legitimate joint-meaning testbed should be an ordered source where both local dynamical structure and broader organization can be observed. The weekly SCC25 cetuximab/PBS experiment is currently the strongest internal candidate because the source itself contains time, perturbation, RNA, methylation, controls, and proliferation.

The joint question is **not** "does scalar chi agree with capital Chi?"

It is:

> Under the same frozen biological trajectory, what information is preserved by a native local dynamical scalar or modal coordinate, what information appears only in the broader RNA/methylation/system organization, and does either representation add task-relevant information after the other is known?

Required falsifiable cases:

- similar scalar coordinate + materially different broader organization;
- different scalar coordinates + similar realized phenotype;
- broader-architecture change without scalar change;
- scalar change without broader-architecture change;
- scalar-only model equals or beats broader architecture;
- broader architecture adds held-out information beyond scalar/modal dynamics;
- neither representation predicts the relevant response adequately.

No scalar formula is selected by this ledger. The existing `rho(T)` G2 object is only a model-class candidate and cannot be silently promoted into biological chi.

## 5. New external challenges that require computation

### CKA debiasing challenge
Historical C1 remains frozen. A new bounded post-result robustness test should compare the existing aligned-vs-patient-null effect using:

- current linear CKA;
- debiased linear CKA / unbiased-HSIC-normalized analogue where mathematically valid;
- RV2 / diagonal-removed cross-product similarity as an independent sensitivity.

The target is categorical robustness of the **alignment effect**, not preservation of the same numerical CKA scale.

### Composition challenge
Before calling any cross-omic result composition-independent, compare the current two-covariate projection with at least one independently justified deeper TME deconvolution sensitivity. Method choice must be frozen from a task-specific benchmark/literature pass, not selected from favorable GRI outputs.

### Source-native SCC25 challenge
For temporal description/representation, the published source's CoGAPS analysis is a required native comparator. For one-step transition prediction, DMD/DMDc/state-space and the already-frozen persistence/mean baselines remain the fair same-question comparators. These functions must not be conflated.

### Multi-omics standard-toolkit challenge
Before any capital-Chi "adds value" claim, compare the frozen task against simple and established integration routes. Candidate families are recorded separately; the decisive comparator set is not selected here.

## 6. Current disposition

The external pass does **not** collapse the program. It changes the claim geometry.

What survives:

- reproducible static cross-omic organization is a legitimate empirical target;
- ordered SCC25 data are legitimate temporal evidence;
- a multirepresentational architecture is a legitimate hypothesis;
- negative/refusal results remain informative;
- internal predictive results remain internal results.

What is not externally earned:

- a universal biological scalar chi;
- a cancer unity boundary;
- CKA as a stability quantity;
- capital Chi as a proven ontology;
- adequacy of purity + leukocyte as full composition control;
- Hallmarks as a privileged feature universe;
- capital-Chi added value over the standard multi-omics toolkit;
- external predictive or clinical utility.

The investigation therefore returns to **reality-facing qualification**, not to re-litigating arithmetic that has already passed.
