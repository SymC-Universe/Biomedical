# GRI Chi_bio A/B/C pre-outcome recommendation

**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Status:** recommendation only, not a scientific freeze  
**Candidate family:** approved G1-S1-L3  
**Chi_bio:** `NOT_ADMITTED`

## Recommended bundle

```text
A3: G2 conclusions must agree materially across r=2 and r=3;
    otherwise return REPRESENTATION_DEPENDENT_NO_TRANSFER.

B3: G1 uses an externally sourced signed-regulon universe plus a
    prospectively frozen low-dimensional rule. CollecTRI is the leading
    prior source; DoRothEA is retained as representation sensitivity.

C3: withhold normalized empirical G1 until restoration is independently
    resolved. Do not invent a common beta to create a unity coordinate.
```

No real Chi_bio value, SCC25 treated candidate trajectory, proliferation response, Atlas placement, or cancer outcome was used for this recommendation.

## Why A3

Both r=2 and r=3 are mechanically feasible under the short-term SCC25 shared-transition architecture. Rank-neutral synthetic pilots verify both without comparing their spectral outcomes to choose a winner. A3 therefore turns rank dependence into a falsifier rather than a tuning opportunity.

## Why B3

A transcriptome-scale operator is not identifiable in the available temporal systems without dominant regularization assumptions. A fixed signed-regulon prior provides interpretable external regulatory coordinates, but the full TF universe is still too large. The low-dimensional rule must therefore be frozen prospectively from provenance, algebraic support and synthetic identifiability rather than selected from favorable SCC25 or TCGA behavior.

CollecTRI currently leads for further source qualification; DoRothEA remains a materially different evidence-tiered sensitivity. Exact exports must be versioned and byte-hashed before use. The existing provenance registry and export preflight select no TFs and compute no activities.

## Why C3

The G1 hardening program shows that unity is exact under common scalar restoration and a separately licensed symmetric generalized-eigenvalue subclass, but not for arbitrary heterogeneous directed restoration. Two targeted SCC25 source passes found gene-specific RNA-stability evidence but no transcriptome-wide restoration source sufficient to identify a general restoration operator. C3 preserves G1 as the primary theoretical family without fabricating the missing decomposition.

## Approval scope

Approval of A3/B3/C3 would authorize freezing the rank-robustness rule, locking exact regulon source bytes/provenance, developing the low-dimensional B3 rule using only pre-outcome criteria, and using G2 as the empirical temporal comparator while G1 normalization remains withheld.

It would **not** yet authorize an exact G1 regulon panel, a normalized empirical G1 value, biological unity interpretation, or Chi_bio admission.

Even after bundle approval, the exact B3 low-dimensional state rule remains a later material freeze if multiple defensible rules survive source/rank/synthetic-identifiability audits.
