## 3. Distance to the recovery hypothesis

The hypothesis:

> a malignant state is characterized in part by reduced ability of a perturbed cellular regulatory system to reclaim a prior or admissible organized state

is closer than scalar chi admission, but it is still a dynamical claim.

Minimum new evidence required:

### R1. Recovery-capable dataset
A system must contain:
- a defined pre-perturbation state;
- perturbation identity/dose/duration;
- response under perturbation;
- perturbation removal or a scientifically equivalent recovery interval;
- repeated post-removal measurements sufficient to distinguish first reclaim, sustained recovery, reorganization and failure to reclaim.

### R2. Native recovery coordinates
Before applying SymC language, freeze native-domain return metrics such as:
- state-distance-to-reference;
- module/program recovery;
- proliferation/function recovery where available;
- hysteresis/rechallenge behavior where available.

### R3. Architectural prediction
Prospectively test whether pre- or peri-perturbation local/modal/system architecture predicts:
- recovery probability;
- return time/order;
- final-state distance;
- reorganization versus restoration;
- repeated-perturbation behavior.

### R4. Independent confirmation
At least one materially independent system/source must test the same frozen recovery relation before a broad oncology stability claim is promoted.

**Assessment:** this is a focused new investigation, not a minor extension of the current manuscript.

## 4. Additional distance to a scalar biological chi

Even if the recovery hypothesis survives, scalar `chi_bio` still requires additional admission:

### C1. Licensed scalar derivation
A dimensionless scalar must arise from a biologically meaningful native model. It may not be normalized toward 1 after viewing outcomes.

### C2. Identifiability
The scalar must be estimable with bounded uncertainty under the available sampling, and its required restoration/dynamic terms must be independently identifiable.

### C3. Representation robustness
Material interpretation must survive justified state/rank/basis choices or explicitly narrow to a representation-specific scalar.

### C4. Incremental value
The scalar must add held-out information beyond strong simple/native biological predictors for the same question.

### C5. Boundary test
If a critical boundary such as chi=1 is proposed, that boundary must be frozen before decisive outcome inspection and allowed to fail.

### C6. Transport
The scalar and its interpretation must transport to a materially independent biological system.

**Assessment:** admission of scalar biological chi is at least one full new P0-D/P0-Q research cycle plus independent confirmation. It is not one remaining computation.
