# Chi Admission Rules

These rules govern admission of the **dynamical response coordinate `chi_dyn = Gamma / (2 |Omega|)`** in the biomedical branch. They do not define `chi` universally across every possible generator or domain.

Program-level interpretation is locked in:

`CHI_SCALAR_MODAL_CONGLOMERATION_FIREWALL_20260830.md`

No biological chi coordinate of any class is currently admitted in GRI v2.

A quantity can enter the biomedical registry as an operational **dynamical-response chi coordinate** only if every hard gate below is true.

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| G1 | Numerator is a genuine dissipative/decay/recovery/relaxation rate. |
| G2 | Denominator term is a genuine intrinsic restoring/response frequency or compatible local rate. |
| G3 | Both quantities have compatible inverse-time dimensions before forming the ratio. |
| G4 | Both refer to the same projected biological coordinate/mode. |
| G5 | Measurements/calibrations are independently meaningful; one is not algebraically reconstructed from the other. |
| G6 | Neither quantity is derived from the downstream outcome later claimed to be predicted. |
| G7 | The factor of 2 and any other scaling arise from the governing model or a prospectively frozen calibration, not empirical tuning toward 1. |
| G8 | The model assumptions needed to interpret scalar Gamma and Omega are stated and checked, including relevant memory/timescale issues. |
| G9 | Uncertainty is propagated to `chi_dyn` and regime labels are withheld if uncertainty spans relevant boundaries. |
| G10 | The mapping is tested on data with actual temporal/perturbational information before dynamical language is promoted. |

Failure of any hard gate means the quantity remains an ordinary atlas feature or candidate proxy. It is not admitted as `chi_dyn`.

## Other possible chi constructions

A future scalar coordinate generated by a different governing model is not automatically rejected merely because it is not Gamma/(2 Omega). It must instead receive its own **prospectively frozen**:

- generator and derivation;
- dimensional or normalization justification;
- interpretation and domain of validity;
- uncertainty model;
- construction-aware nulls;
- admission and falsification gates;
- firewall against importing meaning from `chi_dyn` or any other chi class.

Until those requirements are met, the quantity should retain a descriptive name rather than be labeled chi.

## Scalar / modal / conglomeration firewall

Admission of any scalar chi coordinate does not establish modal similarity, modal inheritance, conglomeration-level organization, biological optimum, treatment state, or whole-system mechanism. Those require their own measurements and tests.

Likewise, modal or conglomeration evidence does not by itself license a scalar chi coordinate.

## Explicit classification of CV/2

`CV/2` fails G1, G2, G3, G4, and G10 as a direct `chi_dyn` coordinate. It is therefore permanently classified in this branch as `historical_comparator`, unless a future independent calibration establishes a specific relationship to an admissible dynamical quantity. Such a calibration would make CV/2 a possible estimator input, not retroactively turn the historical formula itself into chi.
