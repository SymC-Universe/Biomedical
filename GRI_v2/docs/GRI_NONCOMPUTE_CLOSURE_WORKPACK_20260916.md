# GRI non-compute closure workpack

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Scope:** work that can advance without waiting for a scientific computation or making a new science-changing choice  
**Program baseline:** GOM v0.8.0 plus project-local GRI controls

## Purpose

GRI has enough frozen evidence that substantial closure work can proceed while computations or future scientific choices remain unresolved. This workpack prevents the program from idling merely because one lane is computationally or scientifically blocked.

The rule is simple: mechanical, provenance, editorial, reproducibility, evidence-mapping, and qualification-scaffolding work can continue. A new state representation, model family, threshold, biological boundary, or confirmatory endpoint cannot be chosen merely to keep the queue busy.

## Immediate safe sequence

### 1. Evidence-to-manuscript reconciliation

For every manuscript section, trace each substantive sentence to one of:

- a frozen artifact/result;
- a project-local freeze/protocol;
- a primary literature source;
- an explicitly labeled inference/discussion statement.

Unknown values remain placeholders or are omitted. They are not recovered from memory.

### 2. Figure rebuild before cosmetic polishing

The old figure language creates a larger claim-ceiling risk than imperfect prose. Build an evidence ledger first, then regenerate only panels whose inputs and captions can be sourced. Historical `Critical Failure`, `Forensic Capture`, and unresolved `Energy Omega` language remains retired unless a native derivation later earns it.

### 3. Methods and reproducibility map

Map the surviving paper claims to exact code/config/freeze/result paths. Distinguish:

- runnable current code;
- historical reproducibility code;
- source/provenance probes;
- deterministic validation/synthetic checks;
- scientific execution that is intentionally closed or refused.

### 4. External-confirmation preparation without outcome opening

Complete source identity, pairing, modality, ordering, and availability checks for candidate cohorts. Do not score a GRI state or inspect the decisive outcome until a P1 task is prospectively frozen.

### 5. Direct experiment design space

Design experiments around unresolved physical/biological questions before literature collision where GOM permits, but stop before one design becomes the official experiment if that choice fixes a new model, endpoint, threshold, or representation.

### 6. Cold-audit readiness

A future independent reader should be able to answer:

- what was known before each decisive result;
- what was frozen;
- what ran;
- what failed/refused;
- what was changed afterward and why;
- which evidence is independent;
- which claim each artifact supports;
- how to reproduce or at least verify the result without reconstructing it from chat history.

- MFR-14 readiness scaffolding;
- native-comparator inventory;
- evidence-independence grading;
- direct-experiment opportunity design;

- stale liveness detection;
- reviewer navigation and submission checklist preparation.

## Hard stop list

Do not autonomously:

- choose externally fixed modules, a dynamics-first/modal state, a high-dimensional regularized operator, or G4 as the replacement state lineage;
- select dimensions, penalties, features, transformations, thresholds, or restoration constants after seeing favorable outcomes;
- reopen the refused compact B3 route under a cosmetic rename;
- convert the G2 unit circle into a biological `Chi_bio = 1` boundary;
- designate a candidate dataset as decisive P1 and inspect its outcome without a prospective MFR-14 freeze;
- merge Stage D science into the current Chi branch without explicit reconciliation;
- merge PR #4 or declare the manuscript submission-ready without an explicit freeze.

## Closure criterion for this workpack

This workpack is complete when the remaining unresolved items are either:

1. waiting on a frozen computation;
2. waiting on an externally verifiable source/identity fact;
3. awaiting a genuine scientific choice;
4. deliberately deferred with reason and evidence role recorded.

At that point, continued editorial motion would no longer increase scientific readiness and the program should wait rather than manufacture work.
