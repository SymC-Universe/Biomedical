**Manuscript ID:** BIOSYS-D-26-00267  
**Revised title:** Recurrent Methylation Organization Across Human Cancers: Context-Dependent Methylation-RNA Coupling  
**Article type:** Research article  
**Corresponding author:** Nate Christensen  

## Suggested keywords
DNA methylation; transcriptomics; tumor-normal comparison; cancer systems biology; representation similarity; molecular covariance

## Highlights
- Frozen cancer-level analyses identify recurrent methylation organization across 32 TCGA cancers.
- Prospectively frozen tumor-normal controls show reproducible weakening of RNA and methylation organization in tumor.
- Independent prostate data reproduce H1 status on 450K and EPIC; post-result attacks preserve H1 after measured composition/TSS structure, while race is insufficient to explain the external effect.
- Cross-layer H2/H3a effects show no detectable external transport and remain context/representation limited.
- The revision makes no causal, recovery-dynamic, treatment-response, or clinical-utility claim.
