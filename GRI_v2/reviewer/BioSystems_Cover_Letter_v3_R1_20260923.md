# Cover Letter

**Round-1 adversarial synchronization:** 23 September 2026

**Re: Revised manuscript BIOSYS-D-26-00267**  
**Revised title:** *Recurrent Methylation Organization Across Human Cancers: Context-Dependent Methylation-RNA Coupling*

Dear Editor,

Please find the substantially revised manuscript BIOSYS-D-26-00267.

This revision is not a cosmetic rewrite of the submitted paper. During the rebuild, several interpretations that did not survive direct scrutiny were withdrawn, including the historical CV/2 biological-damping interpretation, a universal `chi = 1` cancer boundary, the Warning/Confirmation/Collapse sequence as a temporal or diagnostic progression, causal substrate-capture language, and treatment-state claims. The revised manuscript now focuses on static molecular architecture and the limits of its transport.

The current evidence has three central layers.

1. **TCGA molecular architecture.** Across 32 cancers, within-methylation organization (H1), methylation-RNA patient geometry (H2), and patient-specific Hallmark correspondence (H3a) were tested separately under frozen constructions. The canonical H1 null is now stated explicitly to be an independence/construction floor. During final post-result adversarial verification, H1 remained positive in 30/30 composition-complete cancers against a stronger null preserving linear ABSOLUTE-purity and methylation-derived leukocyte structure, and again in 30/30 after additionally preserving TCGA Tissue Source Site-associated mean structure. A conventional PCA PC1-fraction summary showed the same 30/30 direction against that stronger null.

2. **Prospectively frozen tumor-versus-adjacent-normal controls.** All three RNA architecture coordinates were lower in tumor in 12/12 primary cancers, with same-participant sensitivity preserving the direction in 13/13, 13/13, and 12/13 cancers. These are reported as **unadjusted tissue-state contrasts**: the tumor purity variable is not symmetric in adjacent tissue, and the exact methylation-derived leukocyte source did not provide sufficient type-11 coverage for a symmetric adjustment. A Hallmark-breadth audit showed that the direction is broad rather than concentrated in a small immune-module subset. Methylation H1 was lower in tumor in all five eligible multiomic cancers, but the exact five-cancer sign-test resolution (p=0.0625; BH q=0.125) is stated explicitly.

3. **Genuinely independent external testing.** Before external molecular outcomes were opened, a prostate transport test was frozen using GSE237995 RNA-seq with GSE262522 450K and GSE262524 EPIC methylation. Primary H1/H2/H3a were computed on tumor samples only. H1 transported on both methylation platforms. H2/H3a showed no detectable external transport. Their small null-compatible point estimates reverse sign across platform/cohort subsets, which triggers the frozen procedural label `P1_REPRESENTATION_DEPENDENT` but is not interpreted as evidence of a real platform-specific effect. A post-result B=9,999 precision extension of the identical deterministic null stream retained H1 at p=0.0001/BH q=0.0003 on both platforms while H2/H3a remained unresolved; the original P1 classification was not changed.

The external H1 effect is substantially larger than the TCGA median effect and is therefore not presented as magnitude replication. A source-declared race sensitivity did not explain the effect, while a metadata-only audit found no additional source-identifiable age, sex, center/site, plate, Sentrix, array-position, or scan-batch field satisfying the prespecified adjustment rules. Unmeasured technical and latent biological covariance remains a stated limitation.

The revised title was narrowed accordingly. The externally supported layer is within-methylation organization; cross-layer methylation-RNA correspondence is retained as a recurrent TCGA-internal finding whose external transport is context dependent.

The P0 held-out branch was also narrowed after final review. Its 47-predictor-versus-2-predictor comparison does not isolate Hallmark-specific incremental value, and only 19 cancers satisfied the frozen all-partition eligibility rule, below the fixed 24-cancer pan-cancer promotion floor. P0 has therefore been removed from the Abstract's central evidence and is retained only as a bounded internal observation rather than defended through a new post-FINAL experiment.

Several major corrections and analyses were already underway before the reviewer-response closure. The public GitHub repository `SymC-Universe/Biomedical` preserves dated prospective freeze files, source identities, code, workflows, result artifacts, failed branches, and post-result audits so that author-initiated work can be distinguished from reviewer-driven additions. The editable manuscript and supplement remain private authoring files, but the scientific chronology and decision rules are publicly inspectable.

The relevant cancer-regulation references suggested by Reviewer 1 were incorporated with narrow claim limits. The suggested engineering-control references from Reviewer 2 were considered but not added because the revised manuscript withdraws the control-theoretic cancer analogy and no longer makes the scientific claim to which those papers would be relevant.
