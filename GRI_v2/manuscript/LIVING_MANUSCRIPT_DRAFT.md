## Working title

**Regulatory Stability Architecture Across Human Cancers: A Multiscale, Multiomic Analysis of Scalar, Modal, and System-Level Organization**

*Title is provisional and may change before submission.*

## Abstract — working draft

Cancer transcriptomes exhibit reproducible organization across molecular scales, but a single scalar summary can obscure the distinction between local variability, modal structure, cross-layer organization, and temporal response. We therefore rebuilt the Gene Regulatory Instability (GRI) program as a staged measurement framework that preserves scalar, modal/vector, and conglomerate/system views separately and subjects candidate stability coordinates to explicit admission and refusal rules. Across the static Pan-Cancer analysis, Hallmark-level RNA organization remains reproducible after sample-size calibration and is only partly explained by measured composition and admitted genomic burden coordinates; orthogonal protein/phosphoprotein measurements retain patient-aligned cross-assay information. Genome-wide methylation adds a distinct regulatory/substrate layer but does not independently license a dynamical interpretation of the historical `chi_RNA = sigma/(2 mu) = CV/2` proxy. Temporal SCC25 analyses further show that a bounded low-dimensional transition architecture can be estimated in a chronic weekly experiment, while short-term and representation-dependent limits prevent promotion to a universal biological stability coordinate. A prospectively controlled `Chi_bio` admission program therefore does not currently admit a system-level scalar or biological unity boundary. Instead, the combined results support a layered regulatory stability architecture in which functioning regions and failure boundaries are mapped jointly, and in which scalar compression is retained only when its governing model and identifiability are independently justified.

**Current claim ceiling:** P0-D/P0-Q architecture and qualification evidence. No clinical utility, causal stability mechanism, universal biological Chi, or `Chi_bio = 1` boundary is claimed.

## 1. Introduction — working draft

Cancer is commonly represented through scalar burden measures, pathway scores, molecular subtypes, latent embeddings, or network summaries. Each representation can be informative, but none is guaranteed to preserve the same biological object. A scalar may summarize variation while concealing the modes that carry it; a modal decomposition may describe internal organization without capturing cross-layer context; and a system-level or conglomerate view may retain architecture that cannot be compressed without loss. The central problem is therefore not simply to identify another score, but to determine which representations remain valid under the measurement process, which relationships survive independent challenges, and where compression ceases to be scientifically coherent.

The original GRI lineage explored an RNA-derived quantity algebraically equivalent to `CV/2`. That historical quantity remains reproducible as an operational transcriptomic proxy, but algebraic resemblance to a damping-ratio expression is insufficient to establish that its numerator and denominator are independently measured dynamical rates. The current framework therefore separates descriptive proxy status from mechanistic admission. Static cross-sectional ordering is not used to infer temporal progression, recovery, damping, irreversibility, exceptional points, or a biological critical boundary unless independent temporal evidence and a native model license those interpretations.

The revised program asks a broader question: what stability-relevant architecture can be resolved reproducibly across cancer systems when scalar, modal/vector, and conglomerate/system organization are measured together, and under what conditions can any compressed coordinate be admitted without circularity? This motivates three paired objectives: a Function Map identifying supported operating regions and relationships; a Limit Map identifying refusal, non-identifiability, representation dependence, and failure boundaries; and a staged admission program for any candidate biological Chi that prevents the Atlas or downstream outcomes from defining the quantity being tested.

## 2. Conceptual architecture

### 2.1 Scalar, modal/vector, and conglomerate/system views

The present framework treats scalar, modal/vector, and conglomerate/system descriptions as complementary starting representations rather than an exhaustive decomposition of biological stability.

- **Scalar coordinates** summarize a bounded property under an explicit measurement definition.
- **Modal/vector structure** resolves the modes, eigenspaces, subspaces, or distributed carriers underlying that organization.
- **Conglomerate/system structure** captures integrated cross-module, cross-layer, and embedded organization that may not reduce to a single coordinate.

A scalar is not promoted because modal or conglomerate evidence appears favorable, and disagreement among the three views remains visible as information rather than being averaged away.

### 2.2 Function Map and Limit Map

Every promoted result is paired with its functioning regime and its known boundary of validity. A positive result may therefore coexist with a refusal elsewhere in the same domain. This design prevents the program from treating ordinary functioning as universal or interpreting a failed representation as evidence that the underlying biology lacks structure.

## 6. G1 restoration identifiability: closing the remaining normalization assumption

### 6.1 Why restoration matters

The theoretical G1 route separates regulatory interaction from restoration/decay. Standard gene-regulatory ODE formulations can represent transcript degradation as a negative diagonal contribution distinct from off-diagonal regulatory interactions. Chen et al. explicitly describe mRNA decay rates as diagonal terms and regulatory effects as off-diagonal Jacobian terms in a GRN stability analysis [1]. This supplies a biologically defensible **structural decomposition**, but it does not by itself supply SCC25-specific numerical restoration rates.

The current SCC25 temporal program estimates a discrete transition operator `T`. Where a continuous-time embedding is admissible, the relationship

`T = exp(J Delta t)`

means that the frozen temporal evidence constrains an effective generator `J`. However, `J` combines regulatory, self, degradation/restoration, and other represented effects. Therefore the existing G2 result constrains the **combined generator** without uniquely identifying the decomposition required for normalized empirical G1.

### 6.2 Condition-matched inference from existing SCC25 RNA-seq

A potentially important route is to ask whether the existing SCC25 time-course sequencing retains enough precursor/mature RNA information for kinetic inference. INSPEcT− was developed to infer RNA synthesis, processing, and degradation kinetics from steady-state or time-course total RNA-seq without requiring metabolic labeling [2]. Its model explicitly uses premature and mature RNA dynamics rather than treating intronic signal as a simple synthesis proxy.

If the SCC25 source files retain the required RNA-seq structure, INSPEcT− or a comparably validated kinetic method could provide **condition-matched inferred degradation rates**. Such estimates would reduce the restoration assumption substantially, but they would not constitute fully independent validation because the kinetic estimates and the temporal operator would derive from the same biological experiment. The manuscript therefore distinguishes condition matching from evidentiary independence.

If the available SCC25 source is only a processed expression matrix lacking the necessary precursor/mature information, this route must be refused rather than reconstructed from downstream summaries.

### 6.3 Independent kinetic evidence as bounds

Genome-wide RNA-stability assays provide a second evidence role. BRIC-seq determines transcript half-lives by following BrU-labeled RNA decay over time and can measure RNA stability transcriptome-wide [3,4]. These measurements are valuable for defining biologically plausible degradation ranges and for rejecting inferred restoration regimes that are grossly inconsistent with mammalian RNA kinetics. They are not automatically SCC25-specific values and therefore are treated as **independent kinetic bounds**, not direct substitutions.

SCC25 itself has historical evidence that transcript stability is measurable and condition-dependent. Saunders et al. measured E2F1 mRNA stability in SCC25 and showed that its response differed from normal keratinocytes [5]. This establishes biological relevance of post-transcriptional decay in the cell line but is sparse, gene-specific evidence and cannot identify a genome-wide restoration operator.

### 6.4 Prospective inverse-identifiability test

The restoration problem is therefore posed prospectively as an inverse problem. The frozen G2 operators define an admissible effective-generator family; only biologically justified restoration structure is imposed; condition-matched kinetic inference is used if the raw SCC25 data support it; independent RNA-stability measurements constrain plausibility; and the entire allowed restoration family is propagated through G1.

The decisive criterion is **invariance**, not agreement with unity. If materially different restoration operators compatible with all available evidence produce the same G1 conclusion within uncertainty, G1 may become identifiable within that stated validity regime. If plausible restoration choices materially change the normalized G1 inference, the correct result remains `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`.

Until that analysis is completed, the strongest manuscript statement is:

> The present SCC25 temporal data constrain an effective regulatory generator but do not yet directly identify its genome-wide restoration component. We therefore treat restoration as a bounded inverse-identifiability problem rather than assigning a common decay constant. Condition-matched kinetic inference from the existing RNA-seq, where technically supported, and independent transcript-half-life measurements are used to narrow the admissible restoration family without tuning the normalization toward unity.

## 7. Discussion — working points

1. The strongest result is architectural rather than scalar: reproducible static and temporal organization survives while several attempted scalar reductions fail admission or become representation-dependent.
2. `chi_RNA = CV/2` remains an operational proxy unless a mechanistic derivation with independently meaningful dynamical terms is earned.
3. The temporal SCC25 program supplies information inaccessible to cross-sectional TCGA, but its mathematical transition boundaries remain representation-level findings rather than biological criticality.
4. Failure of the current `Chi_bio` candidate is informative because it maps a compression limit rather than erasing the underlying modal/system structure.
5. Restoration identifiability is a tractable remaining mechanistic question because existing temporal operators already constrain the combined generator; the next step is to determine whether RNA kinetics can separate or tightly bound the restoration component.
6. Function and Limit Maps should be reported together so that positive operating regions, representation dependence, refusal, and non-identifiability remain visible in the same framework.

---

## 8. Limitations — working draft

The principal limitations are the cross-sectional nature of the Pan-Cancer core, incomplete independence among some development layers, representation dependence in parts of the temporal analysis, and absence of a directly measured genome-wide SCC25 restoration operator. The proposed restoration-identifiability analysis may reduce the latter limitation but cannot be described as independent measurement if degradation is inferred from the same SCC25 time-course experiment used to fit the transition operator. External RNA-half-life datasets improve biological plausibility bounds but introduce cell-line and condition mismatch. These evidence classes are therefore reported separately.

No result currently licenses a universal biological Chi, a biological unity boundary, a clinical decision threshold, causal regulatory direction, or a claim that the mathematical discrete-time unit circle corresponds to a biological critical point.
