## Editing rule

The manuscript is allowed to evolve incrementally. A new analysis does not require an immediate full-manuscript rewrite, but any result that materially changes a claim, limitation, figure, or interpretation should be reflected either in the integrated draft or in a clearly indexed current section file.

Historical manuscript copies are not authoritative merely because they are older or formatted for submission. Final submission authority will be established later by an explicit manuscript freeze and line-by-line reconciliation.

## Current scientific ceiling

- `chi_RNA = sigma/(2 mu) = CV/2` remains an operational proxy unless a separately validated mechanistic derivation earns more.
- `Chi_bio` is not admitted.
- `Chi_bio = 1` is not a biological boundary.
- G1 restoration identifiability is an active bounded inverse problem under a prospectively recorded plan.
- Static, modal/vector, conglomerate/system, and temporal results retain their individual evidence classes.
