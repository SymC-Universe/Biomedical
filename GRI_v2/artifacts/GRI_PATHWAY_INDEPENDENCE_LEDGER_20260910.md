## Controlling question

For each claim/evidence path:

> Could shared information on this pathway force the agreement being presented as evidence?

Independence is not a single binary label. The dimensions below are assessed separately.

## Grade vocabulary

- `INDEPENDENT`: no shared information on this pathway capable of forcing the decisive agreement.
- `PARTIALLY_INDEPENDENT`: some shared nondecisive transformations/context exist, but decisive evidence is distinct.
- `NON_INDEPENDENT`: shared data/outcome/tuning/path can force or materially bias the agreement.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`: pathway not relevant to the claim.
- `UNRESOLVED`: insufficient provenance to grade.

For Atlas validation, use the canonical v0.7.1 grade `NON_INDEPENDENT_FOR_ENGINE_VALIDATION` where applicable.

## 2. What FINAL_HOLDOUT independence does and does not mean

The FINAL_HOLDOUT result has a real independence property: the decisive held-out molecular targets in that partition were not used to fit the frozen models or P3-v2 selector before opening. This is meaningful internal holdout evidence.

It does **not** supply:

- independent institution/cohort acquisition;
- independent TCGA source lineage;
- independent assay ecosystem;
- independent external prevalence;
- independent platform generalization;
- independent clinical utility;
- temporal independence for progression/recovery claims.

The correct phrase is therefore **untouched internal TCGA FINAL_HOLDOUT under the frozen P3-v2 lineage**, not external validation.

## 4. Atlas-specific path rules

A future Regulatory Substrate Atlas may contain development/historical TCGA entries, but each reference family used decisively for Engine validation must carry the v0.7.1 Atlas grade:

- `INDEPENDENT`
- `PARTIALLY_INDEPENDENT`
- `NON_INDEPENDENT_FOR_ENGINE_VALIDATION`

The Tool may show non-independent development references descriptively. It must not count them as independent validation.

## 5. Rare-natural-testbed independence

For a future `RARE_NATURAL_TESTBED` used in P1:

- rarity/eligibility must be determined independently of the Engine result;
- selection cannot use favorable residual, closeness to a desired chi, or agreement with GRI architecture;
- the candidate universe/search route should be preserved where feasible;
- already-viewed rare/extreme cases are P0-D/P0-Q only for rules developed from them;
- future/untouched extremes may test a frozen limit prediction.
