## 2. Limit type distinctions

### 2.1 Representation limits

Representation limits mean the mathematical/statistical representation does not support the interpretation being requested. Examples: `CV/2` as biological chi; raw cross-cancer `Delta_CKA` as a universal bridge; nonlinear-only dependence under the current linear auditor.

### 2.2 Evidence-type limits

Evidence-type limits mean the data cannot answer the question even if the computation is correct. Static TCGA cannot establish temporal inheritance or recovery. Internal holdout cannot establish external generalization.

### 2.3 Data-support limits

Data-support limits include insufficient mapping, sample support, missing covariates, platform incompatibility, or other input conditions that make a requested result scientifically unevaluable.

### 2.4 Comparator limits

A positive comparison against a lower-capacity baseline does not license a broader added-value claim than the comparator actually tests. The current FINAL_HOLDOUT result establishes additional methylation information beyond purity/leukocyte covariates, not capacity-matched superiority of Hallmark architecture.

### 2.5 Tool-qualification limits

A collection of scientifically useful scripts does not equal a P2-qualified Engine. Production qualification requires the Engine to reject known-bad inputs, survive mutation testing, reproduce in a clean environment, expose explicit output/refusal semantics, and bind to an independently versioned Atlas where Atlas use is claimed.

## 3. Boundary versus transition discipline

Current GRI data do not establish a biological dynamical transition surface. The word `boundary` in this map means one of:

- evidence/admission boundary;
- reduction-adequacy boundary;
- engine-scope boundary;
- input-support boundary;
- internal empirical stress region.

Any future claim that one of these corresponds to a biological state transition becomes a new S-class hypothesis and requires its own MFR-14 and untouched temporal/perturbational evidence.
