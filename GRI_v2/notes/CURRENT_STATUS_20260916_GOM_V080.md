## Reader-first current state

GRI is an oncology research program mapping regulatory stability architecture across static Pan-Cancer and ordered/perturbational systems. It is not a validated clinical tool.

The current evidence supports a layered architecture more strongly than a universal biological scalar. Scalar, modal/vector, and conglomerate/system representations therefore remain separate but related views. They are not averaged into a master quantity merely to obtain a biological `Chi`.

`Chi_bio` remains **NOT_ADMITTED**. No cancer-specific `Chi_bio` value, biological `Chi_bio = 1` boundary, clinical threshold, damping interpretation, exceptional-point interpretation, treatment rule, or causal recovery claim is licensed.

The independent chronic weekly SCC25 experiment supported a narrower temporal result. These are transition-operator results. The mathematical unit circle is not a biological `Chi_bio = 1` boundary.
