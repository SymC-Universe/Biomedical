# Scientific Contract: Phase 0C v0.2 Three-Layer Holdout

1. Phase 0C v0.1 is preserved as superseded before scientific execution; it is not retroactively scored.
2. Scalar-spectrum, modal/carrier, and whole-system organizational evidence are evaluated separately.
3. No passing lower layer automatically promotes another layer.
4. No failing lower layer automatically destroys a higher layer if the higher-layer object remains prospectively identifiable.
5. Whole-system organization is represented by a structured tuple, never by averaging the three layers into one scalar.
6. Individual eigenvectors are suppressed when crowding makes the invariant subspace the more stable object.
7. Observable order is not equated with latent order under weak observability.
8. Individual real-pole scalar rates remain refused.
9. Truth is used only after automatic decisions for holdout scoring.
10. No diagnosis, phenotype, treatment, historical neuro-spectrum ordering, chi target, or GRI outcome enters the selector.
11. The GRI C1 result motivates only the methodological separation of layers; it supplies no neural mechanism or threshold.
12. All challenge conditions, adverse outcomes, refusals, and failed fits remain machine-readable.
13. A Phase 0C v0.2 FAIL is preserved; thresholds may not be changed and rescored as the same prospective holdout.
14. Only prospectively passing layers may proceed to label-blind EEG adequacy.
15. No whole-system scalar or preferred chi value may be claimed from this phase.
