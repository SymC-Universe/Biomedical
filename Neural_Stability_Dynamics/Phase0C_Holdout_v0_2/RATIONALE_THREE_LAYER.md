# Why Phase 0C changed before execution

The revision is a perspective correction, not an attempt to rescue or improve a holdout result. Phase 0C v0.1 had not been scientifically executed.

Two independent observations motivated the change.

First, the recent GRI C1 result retained organized methylation/RNA architecture under a workflow that did not use historical GRI or biological chi as inputs. The permitted lesson for this project is methodological: organized dependence between levels can survive without making a preselected scalar the organizing principle. No cancer-to-neural mechanism is inferred.

Second, neural Phase 0B already showed an analogous mathematical warning inside the neural estimator itself. Near degeneracy preserved pole recovery and the joint observable subspace much better than it preserved individual eigenvectors. Weak observability also showed that the observable system may license a reduced structure rather than recovery of every latent component.

Accordingly, v0.2 tests three objects prospectively:

`scalar-spectrum state <-> modal/carrier geometry <-> whole-system organization`

The arrows mean complementary views of one observed dynamical system, not numerical equality.

The whole-system level is not `scalar + vector averaged together`. It is a structured object containing effective order, the observable-subspace projector, normalized channel participation, and normalized relational pole geometry.

The layer-dissociation challenges are the critical new falsification device. They deliberately create cases in which one layer should change while another should remain stable. If the estimator cannot make those distinctions prospectively, the three-layer interpretation is not admitted.
