# NSD Measurement and Inference Safeguards

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: **ACTIVE PROJECT-LOCAL PROTOCOL UNDER GENERAL PROTOCOL v0.8.0**

## Purpose

General Protocol v0.8.0 consolidates the active manual around transferable program-wide rules and moves project-specific equations, measurement conventions, Atlas targets, implementation details, and domain-specific examples into project protocols unless they are required to execute a general rule safely.

This file preserves NSD-specific safeguards that remain scientifically active but no longer belong in the program-wide General Protocol. It does not promote, rescore, or alter any historical NSD result.

## Spectral and dynamical measurement discipline

1. Periodic and aperiodic spectral structure are represented separately where the data and native method support that decomposition.
2. A quality factor and a mode-specific chi derived from the same licensed second-order resonance are algebraically related and are not counted as independent features or independent confirmations.
3. A fitted spectral center frequency is not automatically the undamped natural frequency `omega_0`. Any conversion to `omega_0` must be licensed by the peak model and the measured observable.
4. A fitted spectral width is not automatically a damping rate. A damping interpretation requires an explicit dynamical/lineshape model and control or bounding of materially relevant non-damping broadening. Gaussian-fit width is not converted to damping without a stated conversion model.
5. When a validated dynamical chi cannot be recovered, retain the native spectral quantity or an explicitly classified proxy rather than silently promoting it to exact chi.
6. Complete peak/mode lists, rejected modes, zero-peak states, fit residuals, and unclassified records remain open-channel information rather than being discarded to produce a numerically complete table.

## Scalar, modal, and conglomerate structure

1. Scalar coordinates, vector/modal structure, and conglomerate/system organization are starting representations for reconstructing NSD stability architecture. They are not a complete decomposition of chi and are not combined arithmetically by default.
2. A whole-brain or whole-system `chi_system` is prohibited unless it receives its own derivation, validity regime, uncertainty model, and admission test.
3. Local dynamical identity is distinct from embedded realized behavior. Directed coupling, transformation by connected systems, feedback return, participation, observability, and identifiability remain separately auditable.
4. Weak observability is not physical absence. Strong participation is not identifiability. Identifiability is not equivalent to low finite-sample uncertainty.

## Spatial, hierarchical, and identity preservation

1. Whole-head aggregation may be reported when scientifically useful, but it is not the default substitute for channel-level or regional structure when the question is spatial or networked.
2. Preserve channel, regional, anterior/posterior, hemispheric, inter-regional, and system-level organization at the resolution required by the question before reduction.
3. Trial, session, subject, site, and other nested observational units are not interchangeable. Repeated observations receive aggregation, clustered uncertainty, hierarchical/mixed modeling, or another justified treatment where required.
4. Metadata joins use the identifiers needed to preserve the intended unit of analysis. Ambiguous or unmatched records are quarantined and reported rather than resolved by silent first-match behavior.
5. Absence of an expected peak or mode may remain a categorical phenotype. Do not invent a numerical chi merely to complete a row.

## Structure qualification and clinical inference firewall

1. Structural qualification precedes clinical-label interpretation. Synthetic/known-truth recovery, adversarial testing, and label-blind real-data adequacy come before label-informed clinical comparisons where those stages are applicable.
2. Clinical labels, diagnosis, phenotype, treatment, outcome, desired chi region, or Atlas agreement may not teach the Structural Engine what basic dynamical structure to find.
3. Detection, association, estimation, classification, power, and prediction are distinct outcomes and are reported separately.
4. Cross-sectional disease/control separation is detection or classification, not prediction of future disease, recovery, treatment response, or transition.
5. Recovery, resilience, or intervention claims require temporal or perturbational evidence capable of supporting those claims.

## Neurostability Atlas project-local targets

Where scientifically available and independently sourced, the Neurostability Atlas may retain:

- healthy neurophysiological state distributions;
- age-residualized or otherwise justified normative references;
- dataset/site and acquisition information;
- recording condition;
- channel/topographic organization;
- scalar distributions and exact/proxy classification;
- modal/subspace/participation structure;
- conglomerate/system organization;
- absent-feature phenotypes;
- disease or phenotype cohorts;
- symptom dimensions;
- longitudinal or perturbational states;
- uncertainty, adequacy, identifiability, and provenance;
- Function Map and Limit Map coordinates.

The Atlas remains independent and read-only with respect to Engine construction. A healthy/control reference is not reconstructed separately for each downstream disorder merely to improve apparent separation.

## Baselines and added-value claims

A multidimensional NSD architecture claiming incremental value must be compared against the strongest relevant simple baseline and the strongest fair native standard toolkit under General Protocol Section 1.2. Failure to outperform a simpler representation limits the added-value claim without erasing narrower descriptive structure that remains supported.

No final comparator identity, recovery threshold, confidence/adjudication convention, empirical Atlas reference zone, or P1 scoring rule is created by this file.
