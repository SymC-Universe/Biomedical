# NSD System Model, Current P0 Definition

The NSD System Model is the current formal representation used to ask what dynamical organization is identifiable in multichannel neural time series. Its name does not assert mechanism, causality, a universal dynamical form, clinical meaning, or predictive validity.

Current starting representations:

- scalar/spectral state: complex poles and derived decay/frequency coordinates where the state-space representation is adequate; chi remains withheld unless a licensed second-order factor is established;
- vector/modal state: observable mode shapes and invariant subspaces under crowding;
- conglomerate/system state: observable subspace, channel participation, local observable order and crowding-aware relational spectral geometry where resolvable;
- open channel: refusals, order changes, residual/model-adequacy information once qualified, rejected structures, failed fits and unresolved objects.

These are starting components. The project remains open to coupling, inheritance, emergence, feedback and additional structure if native science and evidence support them.

Current unresolved scientific requirements are tracked in `registries/OPEN_CHANNEL_REGISTRY.json` and must remain visible rather than being filled by assumption.
