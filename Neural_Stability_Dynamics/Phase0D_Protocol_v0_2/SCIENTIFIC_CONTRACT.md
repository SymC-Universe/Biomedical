# NSD Phase 0D v0.2 P0 Scientific Contract

1. Phase 0C v0.2 remains the official prospective FAIL / FAIL / FAIL result.
2. Phase 0D v0.1 remains unexecuted and is superseded before execution under protocol v0.7.1.
3. This v0.2 package is P0 development. It contains no authorized confirmatory holdout.
4. The NSD System Model is not assumed to be mechanistic or predictive. The Structural Engine is its current executable candidate only.
5. No atlas labels, clinical labels, diagnosis, phenotype, treatment, outcome, GRI result, chi value or desired ordering may enter structural selector decisions.
6. Scalar spectral state, modal/carrier structure and conglomerate/system organization are starting representations, not a complete decomposition of chi and not an arithmetic combination.
7. A whole-system scalar is prohibited unless separately derived and validated.
8. Layer C subclaims are reported separately. Unresolved relational geometry is `UNRESOLVED` and yields only a PARTIAL system result, not a full admission.
9. Local order change is preserved as open-channel structure. Until a shared-mode persistence method is justified, unequal-order comparisons are refused rather than forced.
10. Numeric thresholds inherited from v0.1 are P0 regression/calibration values only. They are not P1-frozen under v0.7.1.
11. No new uncertainty margin, model-adequacy threshold, comparator or regime boundary will be invented for convenience.
12. P1 may begin only after complete MFR-14, comparator route, uncertainty handling, multiplicity accounting, evidence-independence map, known-bad qualification and frozen-manifest verification are in place.
13. Method-scope outcomes use the METHOD_SCOPE namespace and may not be advertised as empirical confirmations about nature.
14. Scientific failure, refusal, partial admission and indeterminate status are valid outcomes and are preserved.
