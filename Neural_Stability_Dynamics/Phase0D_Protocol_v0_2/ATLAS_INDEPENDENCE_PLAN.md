# Neurostability Atlas Independence Plan

Status: Atlas not yet qualified. Updated prospectively for General Protocol v0.7.1A.

The Atlas must remain read-only with respect to Structural Engine rules during confirmatory use.

## Pathway-specific independence

Independence is not collapsed into one global label when the claim depends on a narrower pathway. For every decisive Atlas/reference family, record separately where applicable:

- `data_independence`: whether raw observations overlap Engine development/calibration evidence;
- `cohort_system_independence`: whether the same subjects, sessions, systems, or repeated instances appear on both sides;
- `outcome_label_independence`: whether labels, phenotypes, treatments, or outcomes entered estimator/rule construction;
- `parameter_tuning_independence`: whether thresholds, hyperparameters, windows, orders, or inclusion rules were tuned against the reference family;
- `method_independence`: whether the evidence is generated by the same estimator/model implementation whose behavior is being validated;
- `atlas_independence`: whether Atlas content was used to teach the Engine what to find;
- `source_literature_independence`: whether the same source table/extraction/literature record supplies both construction and purported validation;
- `temporal_independence`: whether decisive evidence was unopened when the relevant rule was frozen.

Each pathway may be graded `INDEPENDENT`, `PARTIALLY_INDEPENDENT`, `NON_INDEPENDENT_FOR_ENGINE_VALIDATION`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or `UNRESOLVED`, with a reason.

The controlling question is:

> Could the shared information on this pathway force the agreement being presented as evidence?

If yes, the corresponding independence claim is weakened or refused. Shared non-decisive context does not automatically invalidate unrelated independent pathways.

## Minimum evidence record

Before real-data validation, each decisive Atlas/reference family will additionally record:

- raw evidence source and version;
- research role (`NOMINAL_FUNCTION`, `PERTURBED_FUNCTION`, `BOUNDARY_OR_TRANSITION`, or qualified `RARE_NATURAL_LIMIT`) where applicable;
- whether extraction was blind to Engine results where feasible;
- base-rate/context information for rare natural cases;
- authenticity/confounding checks for rare natural cases;
- overall interpretation ceiling derived from the pathway-specific grades rather than replacing them.

TDBRAIN data used to assess real-data adequacy cannot simultaneously count as independent Atlas validation of that same Engine behavior on a pathway where the shared information could force agreement. Any such reuse must remain visible in the relevant pathway grade.
