# Phase 0C v0.1 status

**Status: SUPERSEDED BEFORE SCIENTIFIC EXECUTION.**

The v0.1 holdout package was frozen prospectively but was not executed for scientific scoring. Before execution, the interpretation of the preceding neural Phase 0B results was revised in light of the recent GRI C1 result: scalar recovery, modal/carrier recovery, and whole-system organizational recovery should be treated as coequal but separable evidential layers rather than making subspace/collective structure a narrow exception to mode-level recovery.

This marker does not alter the historical v0.1 preregistration, frozen rules, design, code, or package hashes. Those remain preserved in commit history as evidence of the pre-execution design.

v0.1 must not be executed and later presented as the prospective admission holdout. The replacement prospective holdout is Phase 0C v0.2, frozen separately before execution.
