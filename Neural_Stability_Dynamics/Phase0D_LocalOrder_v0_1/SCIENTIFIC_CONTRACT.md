1. Phase 0C v0.2 remains an official prospective FAIL/FAIL/FAIL result. Phase 0D is a new estimator version and new holdout, not a rescore.
2. No EEG, diagnosis, phenotype, treatment, GRI outcome, historical desired ordering, or chi value may enter selector decisions.
3. Observable order is local to the record segment being interpreted. Full-record order may be reported as mixture complexity but cannot be imposed on local segments.
4. Layer A concerns scalar spectral state: poles/eigenvalues and their local stability. Real-pole scalar interpretation remains refused.
5. Layer B concerns carriers: observable mode shapes where individually identifiable and invariant subspaces under crowding. Carrier matching is based on MAC/subspace, not on preserving pole values.
6. Layer C is a structured whole-system object, not a scalar. It contains local observable order, observable-subspace projector information, normalized channel participation, and crowding-aware resolved-cluster spectral geometry.
7. Intra-cluster geometry is not scored when crowding makes individual poles non-identifiable. Such geometry is marked `UNRESOLVED`.
8. Partial observability is a valid result. Strong-mode recovery and spurious-mode precision are scored explicitly; no mode may be forced into a result to satisfy latent state count.
9. All three layer statuses are preserved independently. Failure of one does not erase evidence in another.
10. No threshold or selector rule may be changed after Phase 0D holdout execution. Any future change requires a new version and new independent holdout.
11. Claim ceiling: synthetic structural admission only. No EEG, diagnosis, treatment, causality, whole-system scalar, preferred chi, or cross-domain mechanism claim.
