## Important limitation discovered by audit

Noise level and duration are not paired perturbations in v0.1. Each condition receives a new random seed, and that seed is used to generate the latent coordinate rotation, observation matrix, process realization, and measurement noise. Therefore the full grid demonstrates broad recovery across the tested ensemble, but differences between low, medium, and high noise or between 12000 and 30000 samples cannot be interpreted as isolated causal effects of those perturbations.

Phase 0B must use paired base systems: hold A and C fixed across perturbations, share the underlying process realization when appropriate, and vary one challenge at a time.
