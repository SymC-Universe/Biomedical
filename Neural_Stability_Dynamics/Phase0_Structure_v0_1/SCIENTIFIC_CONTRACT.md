# Scientific Contract - Phase 0 Structure Calibration
1. The primary object is dynamical structure, not chi.
2. Scalar spectra and modal geometry are evaluated separately.
3. A fitted eigenvalue does not establish a correct eigenvector/mode shape.
4. A correct-looking spectrum does not establish whole-system organization.
5. No diagnosis, phenotype, treatment, or historical neuro-spectrum coordinate may affect estimator construction.
6. Synthetic truth is generated before estimator output is inspected.
7. This calibration fixes model order to known latent order. Order selection is a separate future gate.
8. Every failed condition remains in the denominator and output table.
9. No failure may be removed because it worsens an aggregate score.
10. Mechanical reruns may repair execution only; they may not alter the frozen configuration.
11. Damping ratio is secondary and reported only for matched stable complex poles.
12. No whole-system scalar is produced in Phase 0.
13. Results are engineering evidence only until prospective acceptance thresholds are frozen.
14. TDBRAIN remains unopened by this package.
